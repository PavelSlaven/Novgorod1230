# Static plant context B06 — bounded source pass

Scope: six general premises for static world knowledge.  Read against current 1295-record runtime bundle: it already has plant root-uptake, plant growth and fungal-hyphae/substrate premises.  Do not duplicate those.  This note proposes neither records nor runtime behavior.

## Admissible premises

| Gap | Supported, qualitative premise | Source | Hard boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wilt/turgor | Wilt is compatible with a water-balance problem, but is not diagnostic: hot wind can outpace root supply even in moist soil; root rot and insect feeding can also wilt foliage. | [UMN: heat stress](https://extension.umn.edu/about/our-stories/news/yard-and-garden-news/keep-gardens-thriving-through-the-heat); [UMN: wilt differential](https://apps.extension.umn.edu/garden/diagnose/plant/vegetable/spinach/plantwilts.html) | One limp leaf/plant does not establish drought, disease, pest, root condition, amount of water, or treatment outcome. |
| Twining climber / hop care | Hops are bines that can grip and be trained around a support; establishment needs moisture without standing water, and transplanting/root disturbance can temporarily weaken a plant. | [Rutgers NJAES: hops](https://njaes.rutgers.edu/fs992/); [OSU Extension: establishment and care](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/sites/extd8/files/catalog/auto/EM9115.pdf) | Do not infer species, a trellis, surviving transplant, growth rate, cone yield, or a fixed water schedule from this general relation. |
| *Rumex acetosa* identification | Identification needs a combination of independent morphology and context, not a leaf name.  *Rumex* contains about 200 species; even close “sorrels” include *R. acetosella*, whose arrow-shaped basal leaves and rhizomatous habit differ from a claimed specimen. | [NC State: genus *Rumex*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rumex/common-name/dock-weed/); [UMass: *R. acetosella*](https://extension.umass.edu/weed-herbarium/weeds/rumex-acetosella/index.html); [USDA SeedImages: *R. acetosa*](https://idtools.org/seedimages/index.cfm?entityID=61862&packageID=2289) | No single leaf proves *R. acetosa*; no morphology-only identification authorizes eating an unknown leaf, safety, flavour, dose, or historical/local presence. |
| Mushroom harvest | Mushroom body and underground fungus/habitat are distinct for harvesting purposes.  Careful cutting or gentle removal can reduce disturbance; raking can damage fungal bed/mycelia. | [USDA Forest Service harvest guide](https://www.fs.usda.gov/media/134170); [USDA PNW: habitat care](https://www.fs.usda.gov/pnw/pubs/pnw_gtr513.pdf) | Do not promise regrowth, yield, edibility, species identity, ownership/access, or that either cutting or picking is universally superior.  Preserve substrate disturbance as a possible causal cost. |
| Seedlings, cold and water | Cold/frost response depends on species, developmental stage, duration and position of growing point.  Damaged leaf tissue may recover when a viable growing point remains; seedlings with shallow roots/reserves can be more vulnerable. | [Iowa State: frost assessment](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/cropnews/2014/05/imbibitional-chilling-and-frost-damage-corn-and-soybean-seedlings); [NC State: stage dependence](https://smallgrains.ces.ncsu.edu/from-the-field-agronomy-notes/) | No general exact temperature/death threshold, species transfer, guaranteed recovery, or yield result.  Evaluate after symptoms/time, not from a forecast alone. |
| Autumn root crops/storage | Harvested roots remain living: warm storage speeds respiration and quality loss; cooling generally slows respiration, but correct temperature/humidity vary by crop.  Cuts/bruises increase water loss and decay risk. | [UAF Extension: root-cellar principles](https://www.uaf.edu/ces/publications/database/food/vegetable-storage.php); [UC ANR: root/tuber damage and curing](https://ucanr.edu/datastoreFiles/234-1937.pdf); [Maine Extension: crop-specific storage](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/4135e/) | No universal cellar temperature, duration, maturity date, harvest quantity, storability or guaranteed decay.  “Cool” must not imply freezing-safe for every crop. |

## Source-use notes

- These are general biological/postharvest constraints, not evidence that a particular plant, fungus, crop, support, disease, harvest, or store exists in a scene.
- Keep diagnosis and identification multi-feature and probabilistic; keep food safety and permission/source questions outside this evidence.
- No candidate is needed for baseline root uptake, generic plant requirements, or fungal nutrition: current bundle already supplies those topics.
