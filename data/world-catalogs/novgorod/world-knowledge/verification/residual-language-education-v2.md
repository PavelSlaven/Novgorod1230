## Approved graphic system

Live RSF text says household writing could substitute `о`/`ъ` and `е`/`ь`,
and place `е` or `ь` for `ѣ`. Current RU/EN state only this graphic fact.
The 1000–1500 applicability is the birch-letter corpus window, not a claim
that every spelling or speaker in every year had that feature; graphics do not
establish pronunciation.

## Approved dated written comparator

RSF describes complete late-XII-century Gramota 1144 and calls its
«от кого к кому» construction traditional for Novgorod letters. RU/EN preserve
the written, late-XII comparator; neither promotes it to speech, delivery,
kinship, or a 1230 rule.

## Approved dated naming practice

RSF's XIII-century Gramota 1157 discussion says women were often called by
forms from their husbands' names and explains Hodzhirova and Spiridanova.
This supports the bounded source-described practice. It does not establish a
complete name generator, social-status system, marriage, identity, or gender
role.
