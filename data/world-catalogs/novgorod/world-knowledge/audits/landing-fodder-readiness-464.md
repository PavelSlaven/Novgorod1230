## Targeted correction: non-horse domestic animals

The cow, sheep/goat and pig unseen-equivalent Core queries each returned
`environment-p1-*`, `fodder-hay-account`, and only
`animal-energy-availability-depends-on-food-derived-nutrients`. A generic
energy requirement does not identify suitable feed or handling. Therefore the
earlier animals-closure recommendation is withdrawn. This is not a demand for
a medieval recipe, a winter store, or one handler per species: one suitable
general biological dietary/handling premise belongs to the already-partial
`wk:biology:organisms` P1 owner and can support composition with historical
animal/hay context.

## Decision

Recommend only `wk:space:river-market` for `covered` at the explicit
category/state boundary above. Keep animals partial for the biology-organisms
P1 dependency, subsistence partial for trapping plus that shared dependency,
and shrubs-grasses partial for the precise non-woody gathering/binding-use
gap. No matrix change follows from this audit.
