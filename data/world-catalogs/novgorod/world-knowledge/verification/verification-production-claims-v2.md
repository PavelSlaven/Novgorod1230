# Production claims: independent reconciliation v2

## Scope and method

This fragment owns **569** claim records. It excludes the four foundation
shards plus `foundation.json` and `historical-population.json`. The 74
physical/life rows remain in this ledger for complete coverage, but retain the
separate auditor's exact row metadata and will be superseded only by that
auditor's final fragment.

For every row, the ledger binds the current canonical claim/localizations,
direct concepts, predicate, evidence and sources through its digest. The
reviewer independently compared current RU/EN wording and applicability with
the cited candidate verdict. An approval is never inferred from a shard name,
an old flag, or a matching substring.

Previously reopened primary sources are cited only where the underlying report
says so. This reconciliation itself did not claim wholesale new source reads.

### Troitsky primary-source reconciliation

The ResearchGate viewer was used to render printed p. 317 of Monk–Johnston
(DOI 10.2307/j.ctvh1dqcg.26). It gives the exact qualified bases: most
identifiable non-wood remains “would suggest” local sourcing; cereals and
millet were “probably” grown in the Poozerie southwest of Novgorod; the named
gathered plants were “likely” obtained from southern decididuous woodland; and
bilberry incidence “would indicate” exploitation of heath areas in northern
woodland clearings. ECO-11a–d are therefore approved only as
inferred/medium source-provenance statements; none establishes a present
plant, field, wood, heath, collection, access or scene outcome.

## Limits

This is a current binding/reconciliation audit, not a substitute for the cited
domain reviews and not a claim that every remote source was reopened today. A
claim/localization/concept/predicate/evidence/source edit changes its digest
and must receive a new independent verdict.
