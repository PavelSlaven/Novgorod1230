# Species-specific winter dormancy: brown bear and common frog

Status: research candidates only; no production approval or historical-presence claim.

## Sources read

1. U.S. National Park Service, [*Brooks Range brown bears spend most of the year in their dens*](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/brooks-range-bear-denning.htm), section **“Denning chronology in an Arctic brown bear population”**. The article identifies brown bear as *Ursus arctos*, calls denning an adaptation to winter resource scarcity, and describes the Arctic study population's underground earthen dens, reduced body temperature, slowed breathing, and reduced activity.
2. Transport Scotland, [*Forth Replacement Crossing: Appendix A10.2, Legislation, Conservation Status and Biology*](https://www.transport.gov.scot/media/25891/j11223-a102.pdf), §2.10.3. The common-frog entry states that adult *Rana temporaria* can overwinter in a waterbody.
