approved. These are not an outfit generator: they establish neither a person,
scene presence, wealth, gender, occupation, ownership, nor a complete costume.

## Source extraction note

CL-S2 full PDF was downloaded only after opening its HTML record and PDF in the
browser. `pdftotext -enc UTF-8` exposes pp. 151–154: local X–XIV ryadinki,
the wool/plant-layer hypothesis, XII Nerev coloured wool threads, and the
explicitly tentative gusset interpretation. CL-S5 was fully opened in browser;
its online heading identifies the 1986 book, chapter and pages. No claim above
turns a broad reconstruction, an unpreserved vegetable fibre, or a later hat
into a present 1230 item.

## Author candidate assessment

The initial local pass was insufficient: its cap and belt-fitting report is
XIV–XV and stays unpromoted for 1220–1240. CL-S5 subsequently provides the
approved broad XI–XIII garment, fibre and construction candidates CL18–27 and
CL34–38. They remain conditional Novgorod compatibility (`medium`/
`inferred`), never a deterministic outfit, wealth marker or scene inventory.
Independent verdict remains root-owned.
