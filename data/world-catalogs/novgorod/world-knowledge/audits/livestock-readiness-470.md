## Handling boundary

There is no remaining independently defined P1 **handling** premise. A request
to perform ordinary care on an already-established animal first depends on
animal identity/state, location, access/controller, actor ability, tools,
time and body/causal resolution. Those are world state, skill/semantic planning
and code-owned simulation, not missing historical facts. Existing
`claim:animal-care-needs-presence` preserves that boundary.

A later claim such as a specific restraint, housing, milking, veterinary or
seasonal management technique would need its own factual support if asserted.
That is not a residual prerequisite for the present declared families, and
LB-06-style modern management was correctly not used.

## Decision

Recommend closing the former shared biology P1 dependency: `wk:biology:organisms`
and `wk:env:animals` are category-covered on current evidence, not merely via
horse analogy. Keep `wk:process:subsistence` partial only for trapping. No
matrix status changed by this audit.
