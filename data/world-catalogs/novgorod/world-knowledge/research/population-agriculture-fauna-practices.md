## Fodder and reed boundary

- **Fodder:** I opened a broad East-Slavic ethnographic discussion that says
  hay was stored for winter feed, but it is not a dated medieval-Novgorod
  source.  It is therefore **not a candidate** here.  The compiled faunal
  assemblage supports domestic-animal context, not a pasture, winter fodder,
  herd schedule or husbandry method.  A future claim needs a dated Old-Rus
  documentary, zooarchaeological or archaeobotanical study that actually links
  fodder/seasonal care to the relevant region.
- **Reed:** AFP-02–03 close a useful non-woody-stem/fibre-processing premise,
  but do not establish *Phragmites* use.  The existing universal reed ecology
  remains non-historical.  A separate regional palaeobotanical or excavated
  reed-use source is still required before promoting a reed-specific relation.

## Exclusions

These candidates do not establish exact cultivation technique, sowing date,
retting, yield, livestock feeding quantity, hunting/trapping mechanics,
arrow use, animal welfare, apiary form, bee species, honey safety, wax stock,
or a particular source location.  Those are either unsupported by the two
sources or belong to scene state, access/ownership, safety assessment and
code-owned mechanics.
