# River landing / market interface — evidence candidates, not production

**Scope.** Bounded historical substrate for `wk:space:river-market`: river
landing/harbour categories and the market-to-river interface in Novgorod.  It
does not author a present quay, named landing, route, boat, cargo, market,
owner, controller, capacity, water level, permission or completed transfer.

## Source independently opened

- M. N. Tikhomirov, [*Древнерусские города*, 2nd revised ed.
  (Moscow, 1956), pp. 45–46](http://rusarch.ru/tihomirov1.htm), electronic
  scholarly-library copy.  The relevant full-text passage was read: in the
  Trade-Side Podol, adjacent to the Volkhov and the market, the author calls
  Novgorod's *vymoly* “пристани”; he identifies the late-XII/early-XIII
  **Ustav Yaroslava o mostekh** as naming German, Ivan', Alferdov, Budjatin and
  Matfeev *vymoly*, and says that paved ways led to them from the Great Row on
  the market square.  This is a scholarly reading of a medieval normative/textual
  source, not an excavation of a particular 1230 landing.

## Exclusions

- The source establishes a landing category and its market-side connections; it
  does **not** directly describe a boat-to-shore loading or unloading operation.
  Do not turn the facts above into a mandatory transfer recipe.
- The later archaeological report of a sixteenth-century Volkhv landing was
  located in search and excluded as outside the target period.
- A source on transshipment at the Volkhov rapids in 1270 is relevant to
  boat-to-boat transfer but is not evidence for a Novgorod city landing in 1230;
  it is not proposed here.

## Suggested normalization boundary

If independently approved, keep these as contextual historical relations for
1100–1300 / `region_novgorod_land` with no current-scene implication.  RL-01
and RL-03 are the substantive river-market gap candidates; RL-02 and RL-04 are
optional supporting context and should be omitted if a reviewer judges them
redundant with the first pair.
