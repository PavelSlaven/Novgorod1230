Atomic decomposition permitted by the reopened passages: Rybina 2015 p. 223
describes cutting/piercing birch bark and joining it with bast; p. 224 describes
flexible rod loops bound with cord; p. 226 describes stone weights attached by
leather strips/cord or bark-and-rod assembly. These are attested processes and
input/tool relations, not recipes or universally sufficient instructions.

Production normalization must retain separate historical applicability,
qualifiers and actor knowledge. Evidence about ancient tools may ground
materialization; archaeological counts and modern scholarly interpretations
must not be installed as ordinary NPC knowledge. No quantity, yield, time,
dimensions, legal entitlement or successful outcome is inferred here.
