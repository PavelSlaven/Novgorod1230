## Promotion boundary

WFM-02–04 are source-qualified **modern** ecological observations. These four
pages alone cannot turn them into universal conditions or 1100–1300 regional
facts. WFM-05, WFM-06, WFM-08, and WFM-10 are approved only as the exact
general-reality premises above: `biology_physiology`, `supported_fact`, and
`domain_internal_only`. They do not assert a scene subject, current stock,
historical regional presence, actor knowledge, gathering, or edible outcome.
The active ordinary-materialization boundary may combine such a premise only
with an independently grounded subject; existing Troitsky claims, not these
modern park lists, remain the historical-presence bridge.
