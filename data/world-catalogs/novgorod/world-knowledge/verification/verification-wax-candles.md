# Wax-candle verification

**Status:** independently reopened source; candidate assessment only. No
production authoring accompanies this note.

## Source

State Historical Museum, [*Wax candles and a next-to-skin cross – a complex of
items from a woman’s burial*](https://nav.shm.ru/en/exhibits/1438/), exhibit
1438, opened 2026-09-04.

The page identifies burial mound C-301, Gnezdovo, Smolensk Oblast; second half
of the X century; MSU expedition led by D. A. Avdusin in 1982. It describes
cylindrical candles made from a convolved wax plate whose edges are tightly
pressed butt-to-butt, and channel imprints indicating wicks of several
intertwisted threads.

## Atomic premises

| Candidate | Verdict | Scope / limit |
|---|---|---|
| wax → candle | approve | Direct museum description of the Gnezdovo objects. For Novgorod 1100–1300: broad earlier-Old-Rus compatibility only, `medium` / `inferred`. |
| textile threads → candle wick | approve | Directly grounded by the channel-imprint description of multi-thread wicks. No fibre species, quantity or wick stock follows. |
| candle forming → convolved wax plate with butt-pressed edge | approve | Directly grounded construction description. No universal method, tool, size, yield or successful attempt follows. |

## Exclusions

The find is a named woman’s burial with a cross and its own early-Christian
interpretation. It does not establish a Novgorod 1230 scene, a candle in any
inventory, a default Christian ritual, a burial norm, a person’s religion, or
exact dimensions/quantity for production use.
