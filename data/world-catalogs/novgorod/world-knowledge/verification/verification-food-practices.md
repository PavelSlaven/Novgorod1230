# Independent verification — food-practices candidates

**Scope:** verification of FPR-01–FPR-06 in
`research/population-food-practices.md`; this is not production authoring or
self-approval.  The only production-eligible outcomes below are
`APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS` for the corrected wording, not for a current meal, stock,
tool, place, recipe, safety result, or NPC skill.

## Sources independently read

1. N. A. Makarov (ed.), *Археология севернорусской деревни X–XIII веков*,
   vol. 3, 2009, printed p. 11, **«Результаты»**.  The source PDF was opened
   from the [IA RAS record](https://www.archaeolog.ru/media/series/arch-northrus-village/Arch_northrus_village-3.pdf)
   and page 11 was visually read.  It concerns the rural sites Minino I and
   Minino VI at Lake Kubenskoye, not the city of Novgorod.
2. D. S. Serezhnikova et al., “Ягодные и плодовые растения в питании
   новгородцев (по материалам раскопок на ул. Боркова в 2023 г.)”, *Российская
   археология* 4 (2024), 129–143,
   [full publisher HTML](https://journals.rcsi.science/0869-6063/article/view/277271/ru_RU),
   DOI [10.31857/S0869606324040101](https://doi.org/10.31857/S0869606324040101).
   The full text identifies the Borkova deposit as redeposited material from a
   likely cesspit; its dating does not exceed the first half of the XIII
   century.

## Cross-record constraints

FPR-01 and FPR-02 must not create an all-grain or all-food rule: each names
only its named cereal and stated use/suitability.  FPR-03–FPR-06 describe
process traces or interpretations, rather than a recipe/action whitelist.
The Minino source supports a regional X–XIII compatibility envelope only;
Borkova is the sole direct early-XIII-century Novgorod context in this packet.

All six corrected formulations are eligible for a separate authoring decision
only with the limits above.  This verification does not approve any production
record because none was supplied for exact-record review.
