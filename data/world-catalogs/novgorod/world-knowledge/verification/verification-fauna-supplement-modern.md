Date: 2026-09-04. Reviewer: Avicenna, independent of root’s candidate
selection. The direct Merck page returned 403 through the permitted web
fallback, so the full indexed source text for the same 2025 Merck Veterinary

Source: Kate Creutzinger, [*Behavior of Goats*](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/behavior/behavior-of-production-animals/behavior-of-goats?mredirectid=4136), *Merck Veterinary Manual*, professional version, full review June 2025; peer reviewed by Angel Abuelo, Michigan State University.

- **Goat social groups — APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS.** In **Social Structure of
  Goats**, the source calls the natural organization a fission-fusion society:
  groups merge and split as goats move through their environment, with changing
  group composition. Approved wording: **«Естественные социальные группы коз
  могут объединяться и разделяться при перемещении и кормлении.»** /
  **“Natural goat social groups can merge and split while moving and feeding.”**
  Keep natural-social-group condition. Do not add a present herd, fixed group,
  rank, management rule, exact frequency, access to feed, or a forecast for one
  goat.
- **Goat kid hiding — APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS.** In **Sexual and Maternal Behavior**,
  the source states that after the first day, for approximately a week, kids
  remain isolated and does visit them briefly to nurse. Approved wording:
  **«В ранний период после рождения козлята остаются в укрытии, а мать
  возвращается кормить их.»** / **“Early after birth, goat kids remain hidden
  while their mother returns to nurse them.”** Do not add an exact duration,
  successful nursing, a present kid/mother/hiding place, survival, historical
  occurrence, care instruction, or automatic actor knowledge.

Both are universal modern biology candidates: `biology_physiology`,
`supported_fact`, `domain_internal_only`, high confidence/direct and unknown
typicality. They are not production approval for any unrelated Cornell claims.
