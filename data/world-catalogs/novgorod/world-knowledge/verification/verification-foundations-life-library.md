# Independent verification: foundations of life library packet

**Scope:** independent source and domain review of
`research/population-foundations-life-library.md`, 2026-09-04.  This is not
production authoring or a medical model.  I opened the listed FB2 members
directly in the read-only library and reproduced the cited chapter headings
and relevant passages.  In particular: L1 confirms the digestive, respiratory,
sleep and skin passages; L3 confirms chapters 25, 29, 31, 43 and 46; L4
confirms the cell, virus, metabolism, reproduction and ecosystem chapters.

The approved wording below is universal biology/physiology only.  It does not
establish an actor's body state, infection, diagnosis, treatment, successful
defence, perception, pregnancy, food safety, or a present organism.  Suitable
domain for approved facts is `biology_physiology` (or its current equivalent),
with `supported_fact`, universal scope and `domain_internal_only`; it is **not**
`social_law` or an historical-presence assertion.

not create an actor-facing medical conclusion and may be considered only after
normalization against the then-current pack.
