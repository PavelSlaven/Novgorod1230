# Batch-03 household and care mechanisms

Research candidate only. Eight static premises; no descriptor, runtime, verification, recipe, quantity rule, or clinical protocol.

- PNNL roof guide: layered overlap sheds water; identified defects can have local maintenance. Modern materials/codes are not medieval construction.
- NPS Preservation Brief 5: earth changes with moisture; compatible materials and differential movement matter. Adobe evidence is analogy for daub, not recipe/durability promise.
- [Verheyen, Albrecht, Elgeti, Jekle and Becker (2015)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28455072/) and the fermentation-temperature study: gas production/retention and temperature affect dough development. No exact time, temperature, ingredient, or loaf outcome. Independent review corrected the initial gas-study attribution.
- [AHA/American Red Cross 2024 guidance](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/2024-first-aid-guidelines), Positioning of the Ill or Injured Person, and the WHO CFAR guide: unnecessary movement in suspected neck/back/hip/pelvic injury presents a potential further-injury risk; unsafe surroundings or airway threat change the context. The guidance notes a lack of direct movement studies: the causal claim is therefore inferred/medium, not experimental certainty. No diagnosis, transport, immobilization or treatment procedure. The position-choice claim binds both the environmental-danger and airway sources.

Existing practical-dwelling-roof covers ordinary inspection, not overlap/water-control continuity. Existing fermentation entries do not cover yeast gas retention or temperature-sensitive proofing. Trauma entries remain causal safety premises, not protocols.
