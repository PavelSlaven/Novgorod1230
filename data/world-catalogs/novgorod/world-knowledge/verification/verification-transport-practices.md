# Independent verification — transport practices

**Scope:** read-only verification of TP-01…05 from
`research/population-transport-practices.md`.  No production, matrix, or
self-approval action follows.

## Source check

The cited IIMK publisher record and its [full PDF](https://www.old.archeo.ru/izdaniya-1/vagnejshije-izdanija/pdf/2019Ugra2.pdf/view)
were independently opened.  I then downloaded the linked publisher PDF and
searched/extracted the full 492-page text, including the stated pp. 202–239.

The bibliographic location is real, but it does **not** support the proposed
transport passage.  On printed p. 202, A. V. Andrienko's actual title is
«Новгород Великий в освоении Северных территорий (по материалам фондов
НГОМЗ)».  Its abstract says the publication is a short catalogue of
archaeological finds made from walrus tusk from the medieval cultural layer of
Novgorod; pp. 203–239 are catalogue text and plates for those artefacts.  The
source has no located statement matching the packet's alleged transport
phrases («Главными транспортными артериями…», small boats on narrow rivers,
sledge use in summer, road clearing/maintenance at portages, or horses hauling
boats and cargo).

The source does mention northern territories, rivers and portages in other
authors' discussions in the *conference volume*, but that cannot be silently
attributed to Andrienko's pp. 202–239 or substituted for the asserted claims.
No exact independent source and anchor for TP-01…05 was supplied in the
packet.

| Candidate | Verdict | Finding and limit |
|---|---|---|
| TP-01 | **NEEDS_EVIDENCE** | The cited Andrienko article does not state that riverine waters were principal transport corridors.  A later/other-author statement in the conference volume cannot repair the wrong attribution.  Require an exact medieval source or an academic transport study with page/section before adding a regional corridor relation.  Even then it must not create a 1230 navigable route, water state, boat, landing or permission. |
| TP-02 | **NEEDS_EVIDENCE** | No passage in the cited article supports small craft on narrow river channels.  Do not infer vessel scale, current, passage, crew or capacity from existing boat-form facts. |
| TP-03 | **NEEDS_EVIDENCE** | The claimed wording that sledges carried cargo/passengers in summer as well as winter was not found in the cited source.  Existing sledge-form evidence is not evidence of this seasonal/use relation. |
| TP-04 | **NEEDS_EVIDENCE** | The cited article neither dates XI–XIII portage settlements nor reports their residents clearing/maintaining roads.  Do not create a porter/service role, road, work duty, payment, route or authority. |
| TP-05 | **NEEDS_EVIDENCE** | The cited article has no claim that portage residents kept horses for hauling vessels and goods.  Do not infer a horse, ownership, harness, cargo, tariff or completed haul. |

## Boundary verdict

The five candidates are **not promotable** from this packet.  Their proposed
source/date/region/directness fields are unsupported because the sole claimed
transport source is a mismatched catalogue article.  A future source must be
read in full and preserve its actual scope: a northern-expansion or portage
statement would be historical regional compatibility only, never a modern
navigation model or present 1230 route/transport state.
