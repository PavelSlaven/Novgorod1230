# Independent verification: historical processes

Verifier: root, independent of the research/authoring agent. This is a partial
disposition of the candidate set, not whole-pack readiness approval.

- HP-03 APPROVE. [IIMK, Archaeological News 17](https://www.old.archeo.ru/izdaniya-1/archaeological-news/annotations-of-issues/arheologicheskie-vesti.-spb-2010.-vyp.-17.-annotacii), Savenkova abstract, explicitly lists loom components found in medieval Novgorod. Broad medieval compatibility; no current loom or universal household presence.
- HP-01/02/04 APPROVE only as medium-confidence inferred reconstruction of thread/weaving-device/woven-textile relations, not a directly quoted operational manual. The same abstract studies textile fragments and reconstructs shedding devices. Do not prescribe one loom design for all weaving.
- HP-10 APPROVE. [IIMK, Archaeological News 28](https://www.archeo.ru/publications/journals/arheologicheskie-vesti-81021/arheologicheskie-vesti-28-3521e/annotation), Kurbatov abstract, explicitly discusses leather footwear and offcuts from the IX–X through XIII–XIV centuries. This supports leather-working outputs, not a current workshop.
- HP-13/14 APPROVE with narrow wording. [Oleinikov 2021](https://rosarchras.ru/s086960630013891-9-1/) was independently opened as full article through browser-harness. Its abstract and introduction identify long-bone blanks, trimmed ends and a levelled sliding surface. Retain cutting/levelling; grooves require the author's exact section anchor, not an extrapolation from the abstract.
- HP-15 REVISE to optional fastening. The same article explicitly discusses skates with and without fastening; figure 3 reconstructs footwear on an unfastened skate. No mandatory fastening prerequisite for all bone skates.
- HP-16 APPROVE narrowed to historical use of an oven for oven-baking. [IIMK excavation report](https://www.archeo.ru/field-research/annotations/raskopki-myisovoj-chasti-novgorodskgo-ryurik-12aee) records eight bread ovens in the ditch slopes and a pre-XII context. Continuing compatibility around 1230 must be marked inferred/medium. Do not make an oven a universal prerequisite for every form of bread baking.

HP-05/06/07/08/09/11/12/19 remain pending an exact supporting anchor or a
separate already-approved claim reference. HP-17/18/20/21 should reuse existing
verified food-drying, fish-drying, combustion and pyrolysis records when they
duplicate them. HP-22 is a research-policy boundary, not a world fact.

Rejected overclaims: all footwear requires tanned leather; all fermentation
requires a container; yarn cannot use reclaimed textile fibre. These are not
valid universal facts and must not enter production.

## Backfill independently checked

- HP-22 APPROVE with broad Rus compatibility. The official [KSIA 232 PDF](https://archaeolog.ru/media/ksia/ksia-232.pdf) was downloaded from IA RAS and its printed p. 192 read with a PDF text parser. It explicitly describes hemp stem fibre for cordage, reins and fishing nets and cites XI-century charter evidence for flax/hemp cultivation. This is not a dated Novgorod household inventory. The stem-to-fibre and fibre-to-cord/net relations may be authored separately; no universal material strength number follows.
- HP-23 APPROVE. Official [Northern Russian village, volume 3](https://archaeolog.ru/media/series/arch-northrus-village/Arch_northrus_village-3.pdf), printed p. 69 (PDF page 70), explicitly records XI-century fur arrivals in Novgorod from the Onega and Dvina districts. Broad later compatibility must be inferred/medium; no current shipment, quantity, species or ownership follows.
- HP-25 APPROVE. [Letter 974](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/974/) has a debt entry concerning malt. Correct its conventional date to 1180–1200 with a possible **earlier** shift; the independent extra-stratigraphic envelope extends to the first quarter of XIII century. A 1230-compatible commodity category is inferred/medium, not a proven 1230 brewing event.

HP-24 remains pending a precise blacksmithing page and temporal scope. The
cited book studies the Golden Horde/Muscovite period, so a later Novgorod
knife assemblage alone cannot establish a 1230 workshop practice.
