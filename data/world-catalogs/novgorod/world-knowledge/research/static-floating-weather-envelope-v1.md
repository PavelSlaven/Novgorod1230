# Floating stability and wind-driven rain envelope

This bounded source pass fills two gameplay-relevant causal dimensions absent from the current bundle. It does not establish a particular vessel, building, weather event, method, capacity, condition, or outcome.

## Floating-body small-angle stability

The University of Texas at Austin’s [Angular Stability of Floating Bodies](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/336L/Fluidhtml/node29.html) derives the small-angle case for a symmetric floating body. When heel changes the displacement shape, the buoyancy line shifts. With unchanged submerged volume, metacenter above center of gravity produces a torque that reduces the heel.

`claim:research-positive-metacentric-height-can-right-small-floating-body-heel` retains those conditions. It complements existing buoyancy/displacement claims, but does not make a boat seaworthy or stable under loading, waves, current, wind, damage, flooding, cargo shift, or a manoeuvre.

## Wind-driven rain at an opening

John Straube’s [BSD-013: Rain Control in Buildings](https://buildingscience.com/documents/digests/bsd-013-rain-control-in-buildings), “Driving Rain” and “Drainage/Storage/Exclusion,” distinguishes rain deposition from penetration and states that wind-pressure differences across cladding can force rain across openings.

`claim:research-wind-pressure-difference-can-force-rain-across-building-opening` therefore requires wind-driven rain, a relevant opening or imperfection, and a pressure difference. It supplies neither a building nor a leak or repair outcome, and makes no claim that the modern design measures described by the source were historically available.
