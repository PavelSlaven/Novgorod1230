# Residual government and law research v2

Purpose: add dated institutional relations, not a constitutional simulator.

## Directly inspected

- V. L. Yanin, *Novgorodskie posadniki*, pp. 175, 178, 187, 192. Searched: posadnik/prince court relation; 1218–1220 political interaction; bishop/archbishop title; commercial court. Result: only the mid-XII mixed-court comparator and title-limit are safe. The commercial-court discussion depends on a reconstructed ca. 1220 statute with later revisions, so no general 1230 competence is claimed.
- M. A. Nesin, *Institut novgorodskikh tysyatskikh v XII–XV vv.*, pp. 51–55. Searched: tysyatsky/prince, posadnik, veche, hundreds, military and trade court. Result: 1215 joint invitation; 1218/19 distinction from prince’s representative; 1228/29 violent veche succession; 1230 `po stom` terminology. Later separate tysyatsky court is explicitly not imported.
- NPL 1220–1243, already recorded in v1. Searched: vladyka mediation, prince/veche oath, external action, ends and streets. It supports dated episodes only; no stable jurisdiction.
- Existing Gramota 724. Searched: tribute. It remains a 1160–1180 Yugra comparator, not a 1230 tribute regime.

## Boundaries

- No verified source in this pass establishes a uniform 1230 competence list for posadnik, tysyatsky, prince, vladyka, veche, end, street, or sotnia.
- No five-end date, end assemblies, territorial authority, or standardized sotnia map follows. `po stom` is retained as source terminology only.
- Crime flow remains bounded: an accusation can be historically narrated and violence can follow in a recorded episode; guilt, proof, court, jurisdiction, regular sanction, and remedy need a specific source/scene.
- Exact offices, holder identity, court availability, enforcement, property, external relation, mobilization and outcomes remain authoritative scene state and code-owned mechanics.
