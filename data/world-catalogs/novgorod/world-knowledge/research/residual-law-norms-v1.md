Author: /root. Read directly through browser-harness on 2026-09-05.

Primary text: [MSU, Expanded Russkaya Pravda, Troitsky witness](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/prp_t.htm). Article numbers below are specifically this edition, not interchangeable numbering of other recensions. Existing `source:russkaya-pravda-extended-msu` is reused without modification. [Waugh’s University of Washington introduction](https://faculty.washington.edu/dwaugh/rus/lexintro.html), based on Kaiser, supports early-XIII existence of the Expanded recension and stresses chronological layers and limited institutional reach.

Nineteen candidates add actual documentary relations concerning offense/proof/remedy, monetary and trade obligations, dependent statuses and family property. They do not duplicate existing articles 29/32/45/50 or care clauses 93/96. Applicability 1200–1300 to Novgorod is a medium-confidence comparative inference, consistent with the existing corpus convention, not attestation that every norm was enforced locally in 1230. No numerical law engine, actor status, guilt, office, present court, remedy or outcome is created. The text’s slavery and gender/status distinctions are described, not endorsed. Ambiguous `potok/razgrablenie` terminology is retained without choosing a disputed exact punishment.
