# Ceramic and religion readiness audit — compiled 476

## Scope and boundary

Independent read-only reassessment of only seven materially affected matrix
cells in current compiled 476 corpus: clay/ceramic, stone/soil,
ceramic-stone process, workshop-religion space, religion, law and occupations.
CSP-01--03 and RP-01--03 are treated as independently verified inputs; this
audit neither verifies them nor changes matrix, authoring, production or code.

A category closure needs a composable historical/physical envelope. It does
not need a present workshop, kiln, clay pit, stone blank, abrasive, church,
priest, jurisdiction, actor skill, access, current rite/case or outcome.
Conversely, a source-specific technology or text rule is not silently widened
into a local recipe, universal stone method or operating law.

## Decision

Recommend only workshop-religion for category `covered`, with stated limits.
Keep clay-ceramic and ceramic-stone partial for their one shared universal
firing-transformation prerequisite; keep stone-soil, religion, law and
occupations partial for their concrete historical premises. No matrix status
changes follow from this audit.

## Compiled 479 follow-up: clay firing transformation

The prior shared clay verdict is superseded by current 479 input. The three
independently approved universal claims are now present:

- `claim:clay-dried-replasticization` — pre-ceramic dried clay can regain
  plasticity when mixed with water;
- `claim:clay-firing-structural-transformation` — firing changes clay mineral,
  chemical and crystal structure to durable ceramic;
- `claim:clay-fired-no-replasticization` — ceramic transformation in firing
  prevents water returning it to plastic clay.

Actual exact-focus Core `materialization_support` slices at 1230 /
`region_novgorod_land` returned `supported`:

| Probe focus | Returned relevant claim refs |
|---|---|
| Suitable clay / ceramic vessel: shape, dry, fire, then assess rewetting | `ceramic-novgorod-wheel`, `household-ceramic-vessel`, `pottery-handmade-local-clay`, `pottery-handmade-temper`, `clay-dried-replasticization`, `clay-fired-no-replasticization`, `clay-firing-structural-transformation`, `population-material-clay-drying`, `population-material-clay-plasticity`, `population-material-clay-quartz`, `population-material-clay-steam` |
| Suitable hard-stone blank: limited sawing and abrasive smoothing | `population-stone-weight`, `population-weight-stone`, `hard-stone-abrasive-sawing`, `hard-stone-abrasive-shaping` |

This closes the exact former causal gap. Local clay/temper and earlier
Novgorod wheel compatibility compose with the universal irreversible
firing/rewetting boundary; conditional stone work remains limited to a known
suitable blank and compatible means.

**479 verdict:** recommend `wk:material:clay-ceramic` and
`wk:process:ceramic-stone` **COVERED** at their declared category boundary.
No new residual factual premise is identified for either cell. This does not
materialize a kiln, fuel, clay, potter, stone, abrasive or tool; it gives no
recipe, temperature, rate, exact method or successful output. Those remain
world state and process/mechanics owners. `wk:material:stone-soil` remains
partial for its separate dated ordinary source/use gap.
