## Cross-record boundary

TO-01--TO-05 are production-eligible only in their corrected, limited wording.
They are operational historical context, not a transport action whitelist.
TO-03 remains specifically limited by the loss of part of the physical letter
and must not be expanded beyond its critical-edition reading and translation.

  applicability, the indirect Vasil'ev anchor, and the season/ground/traction/
  capacity/route exclusions.

All four use the approved `supported_fact` shape with regional 1100–1300,
medium/inferred, internal-only qualifiers and their corresponding source
anchors. **Exact normalization: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS passed.**
