# Independent verification: local family and institutional correspondence

Root opened the five primary corpus records with browser-harness, including
their dating, original text and available translations. This review is
independent of the candidate author. These are premises for historical social
context, not proof of a current person's status, belief, obligations or goods.

- FSR-01, https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/9/:
  REVISE then APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The father and kin **gave property to the
  writer**, not “gave her in marriage.” Her complaint says that her husband
  retained those goods, took another wife and expelled her. The expression
  about striking hands is translated as a new betrothal, not physical assault.
  Preserve reported allegations and the request for help; no general marriage,
  divorce or property rule follows. XII-century document, inferred/medium
  broader medieval compatibility.
- FSR-02, https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/7/:
  APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The catalogue identifies instructions to collectors;
  the surviving fragment mentions collection, grivnas and fur counts. It does
  not specify a complete tax, rate or collector's authority. Conditional
  1200–1220 dating has a wider late-XII/early-XIII stratigraphic envelope.
- FSR-03, https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/87/:
  APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The surviving address names priest Drochka and greets
  several recipients; the request itself is lost. It supports clerical
  correspondence and greeting forms, not a parish roster or recovered request.
- FSR-04, https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/112/:
  APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS for the common demand/dispute/city-court premise only.
  Russian and older English translations differ materially on the underlying
  transaction; do not turn it into a definite marriage or livestock-sharing
  contract. Preserve the uncertainty of that detail. The letter proposes a
  settlement or litigation path; it does not establish court availability or
  an enforceable judgment in a scene.
- FSR-05, https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/147/:
  APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The 1220–1240 letter identifies its sender as podvoisky,
  says whitefish are not yet available and promises good *pochestie*. Retain
  the historical term, without defining it as a tax, salary or fixed tribute.
  It can support a stated office/address and an unfulfilled provisioning
  promise, not office powers or an actual fish stock.

Keep source-specific attestations bounded and substantive. Specialist
interpretations remain domain-internal; general documented letter/greeting
forms do not grant individual literacy or doctrinal/legal expertise.
