**Scope:** read-only verification of TRC-01…05 from
`research/population-terrain-resource-context.md`. No production change or
self-approval follows.

## Source independently opened

V. A. Bulkin & I. V. Antipov, [«Об особенностях организации строительства и
строительного производства в Новгородской земле в домонгольское
время»](https://doi.org/10.18688/aa177-4-40), *Актуальные проблемы теории и
истории искусства* 7 (2017), pp. 410–418. The journal record and its
[full OA PDF](https://publ.actual-art.org/aptha/ru/article/download/10572/10572/10572)
were independently opened. Full PDF pp. 410–418 was read; p. 415 was rendered
and visually checked because its embedded text extraction is incomplete.

The article concerns organization of **pre-Mongol monumental stone-church
construction** in Novgorod Land. It is not an excavation report for a raw
deposit, ordinary dwelling, or 1230 scene inventory.

## Boundary verdict

All five relations are admissible only as historically bounded construction
context. They do **not** establish a terrain/raw-material source at a scene:
the source offers no clay pit, sand/gravel collection, limestone exposure,
quarry location, accessible stock, ownership, or permission to remove material.
TRC-01 must preserve its modal `видимо`; TRC-05 must preserve the p. 415
`вполне вероятно` reconstruction. No claim here supports ordinary household
building or a deterministic workforce/production pipeline.

## Contract-normalization check

**PASS_WITH_LIMITS.** The five production records retain the approved
subject-level specialist scope, evidence anchors, `domain_internal_only`
access and directness/confidence: TRC-01 and TRC-05 remain
`inferred/medium`; TRC-02--04 remain `direct/high`. Their `1100–1300`
applicability is a historical-compatibility envelope for a query, not a claim
that every attested project or event occurred throughout that interval. The
specific pre-Mongol/1207 dates and modal limits remain in the approved runtime
wording and evidence. In particular, making TRC-05 applicable only in 1207
would wrongly hide a qualified 1207 historical example from a 1230 query.

This approval does not broaden any current-world inference: the records still
cannot establish a present quarry, material stock, workshop, worker, route,
permission, recipe or building. Regenerated runtime/index artifacts must carry
the same claim refs, evidence, qualifiers and localized limits.
