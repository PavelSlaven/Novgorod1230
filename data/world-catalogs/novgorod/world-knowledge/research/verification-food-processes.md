# Independent verification — food-process mechanisms

Status: universal mechanisms only; all accepted relations are `domain_internal_only`. No recipe, safety guarantee, threshold, preservation duration, material presence or historical availability follows.

- **FP01 — APPROVE:** Heating can damage microbial membranes/proteins and reduce viability. OpenStax Microbiology 13.2, “Heat”, lines 30–33.
- **FP02 — APPROVE:** Low temperature inhibits microbial metabolism and slows growth; exceptions include cold-preferring microbes. Same source, “Refrigeration and Freezing”, lines 67–71.
- **FP03 — APPROVE:** Drying controls growth because cells require water, but surviving microbes/endospores may regrow after water returns. Same source, “Desiccation”, lines 97–101.
- **FP04 — APPROVE:** Added solutes reduce available water and inhibit many microbes; tolerant molds/yeasts remain a limit. Same source, lines 102–105.
- **FP05 — APPROVE:** Microbial growth range is species-specific. OpenStax Microbiology 9.4, “Temperature and Microbial Growth”.
- **FP06 — APPROVE:** Cold-tolerant microbes can remain compatible with food spoilage; no refrigerated-food safety conclusion. Same source, psychrophile discussion.
- **FP07 — APPROVE:** Available moisture is a growth condition. OpenStax Microbiology 9.5, “Water Activity”.
- **FP08 — APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS:** High osmotic pressure can withdraw water/plasmolyze cells; halophiles/halotolerants limit any general result. Same source, “Osmotic Pressure”.
- **FP09 — APPROVE:** Microbial fermentation can convert sugars into organic products. OpenStax Microbiology 8.4, “Fermentation”.
- **FP10 — APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS:** Lactic fermentation produces lactic acid and can alter food medium/proteins. Same source, lactic fermentation discussion; no specific food outcome.
- **FP11 — APPROVE:** Microbial growth and fermentation compatibility differ by oxygen requirement. OpenStax Microbiology 9.2, “Oxygen Requirements for Microbial Growth”.
- **FP12–13 — APPROVE mechanism only:** Lactic bacteria convert carbohydrate to lactic acid/CO2; yeast can convert sugar to ethanol under low/no oxygen. UMN Extension, “Fermentation”; reject all modern method/safety guidance.

Accepted wording must stay conditional: `process compatibility depends_on actual_microbe substrate temperature moisture solute oxygen and time`. It cannot certify edible/safe/stable food.
