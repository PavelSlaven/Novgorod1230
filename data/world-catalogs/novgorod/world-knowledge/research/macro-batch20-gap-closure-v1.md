# B20 resin-on-hand-tool reconstruction

This narrow `editorial_reconstruction` applies only after a resinous residue is already observable on an already present hand tool. Resin is treated as sticky residue: keeping it away from precise handling or trying a compatible available means to remove it is ordinary qualitative physical reasoning. It supplies no solvent, heat, method, tool, timber, present residue, successful cleaning, or historical workshop practice.

It exists to keep the claim honest: the corpus has no direct source that turns resin-contaminated-tool cleaning into a fixed historical procedure. Exact material, access, safety, quantity, time, outcome, and committed state remain owned elsewhere.
