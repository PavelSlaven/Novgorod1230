not a direct assertion about a 1230 Novgorod household.

## Negative result: the requested feeding gap remains open

The chapter was examined across printed pp. 16–24, including its discussion
of domestic remains and carcass-processing bias on pp. 23–24. It contains
**no direct evidence** for winter fodder collection/storage, pasture rotation,
grazing practice, stable feeding, troughs, or a species-specific diet. None
of those premises is proposed here. Inferring fodder from livestock bones,
from the presence of grasses, or from a harvesting tool would overstate this
source.

The next defensible evidence path is an independently read, date-compatible
birch-bark/textual record mentioning hay or feed, or an excavated feeding
feature/implements publication whose context directly supports management;
a rake or other isolated tool alone is insufficient.
