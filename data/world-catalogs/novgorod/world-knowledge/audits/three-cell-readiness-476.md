## Correction: shrubs-grasses discovery versus action-produced result

The prior shrub/grass row wrongly treated a simple bundle made from
**already-present** stems as needing an attested historical reed-use recipe.
That mixes two causal paths.

- **Pre-existing discovery/materialization:** a reed or comparable wet-ground
  stem source needs regional vegetation/source basis. Current Core discovery
  slice returns `common-reed-rhizome-produces-stems-in-wetland-habitat`, but
  that is universal biology, not a dated Novgorod-Land riparian/wetland plant
  occurrence. Meadow and gathered-shrub context does not specifically fill
  that source gap.
- **Action-produced result:** where suitable stems, bast or cordage are
  already in committed context, a player may attempt an ordinary simple tied
  bundle without a historical attestation of that novel exact use. The actual
  Core bundle slice is `supported` and includes
  `construction-bast-vessel-fastening`, `population-bark-bast`,
  `population-processes-hemp-cordage` and
  `common-reed-rhizome-produces-stems-in-wetland-habitat`. Those support
  available lashing/cordage inputs and physical semantic resolution; actual
  suitability, quantity, tool/access, force and result remain state/mechanics.

**Revised verdict:** `wk:env:shrubs-grasses` stays **PARTIAL**, but solely for
one pre-existing-discovery premise: a qualified regional Novgorod/Rus
riparian/wetland vegetation source for reeds or comparable usable stems. It
does **not** need a dated reed-binding use, an exhaustive plant catalogue, a
scene reed stand, or a fixed bundle recipe. No separate stem-flexibility or
cord-binding mechanical gap is demonstrated by current Core composition.
