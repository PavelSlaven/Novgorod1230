# Temporal v4 bounded activity authoring decisions

Status: approved authoring reconstruction for this package.

- Waiting, sleep, untooled manual labor, sacred reading/recitation, meal/rest, and a narrow stable co-located carrier transfer are the minimum profiles required by the returned NPC/place/body/carrier rules.
- The transfer duration is exactly 10 minutes only for a small group, stable footing, exact co-location, valid capacity and no emergency. Any other transfer requires a separate approved profile and never inherits this value.
- Schedule-derived activities seal their exact duration from the difference between already approved exact boundaries. They do not round or use runtime defaults.
- These are gameplay authoring conventions authorized by the user; they are not asserted as measured medieval task durations.
