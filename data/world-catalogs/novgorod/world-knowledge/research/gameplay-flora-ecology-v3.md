## Method and actual-pack baseline

Read `wild-flora.json`, `environment-biology.json`, `environment-ecology.json`,
`fauna-ecology.json`, `fauna-supplement.json`, and `fauna-mammals.json`, rather
than treating 105 families as saturation.  Existing owners already cover:

- `common-reed-rhizome-produces-stems-in-wetland-habitat`, a narrow *Phragmites*
  habitat/growth relation; `white-willow-depends-on-moist-lit-riparian-habitat`;
  hydric soil; river sediment transport/deposition; water temperature; and
  modern Valdai/archaeobotanical contextual limits.
- fungi resemblance, bearberry/lingonberry resemblance, and mezereon toxicity.
  Those are not a non-fungal identification model.
- generic cellulosic plant fibre, hemp/flax origin and water response.  Those
  are material facts, not a wild-source or retting premise.
- selected animal, fish, amphibian, insect, migration and winter facts.  They
  do not form a general weather--plant--food-web response family.

Sources were opened with Browser Harness where reachable.  The Forest Service
pages returned a service block and several guessed USGS paths returned 404, so
they are not evidence.  Every proposed relation instead cites an accessible
government, university, or primary research record.  Modern ecology is used
only for universal mechanism; medieval Novgorod applicability remains separate.

## Composition and unseen-case probes

1. **Spring willow, late-budding tree:** S1 permits different stage timing; resolver must inspect species/state/weather.  It must not leaf-out both because month is May.
2. **Cut reed versus pulled rhizome:** FLO-ECO-03 keeps parts distinct.  A cut stem neither creates a second plant nor proves regrowth rate; a removed rhizome needs source-state transition.
3. **Unknown red berry beside lingonberry:** S6/S7 do not identify it.  No generic colour or resemblance rule produces food/poisoning; retain identification and knowledge gaps.
4. **Flax-like wild stem:** FLO-ECO-05 cannot turn it into flax fibre.  It needs taxon identity and a source for that plant's anatomy/process.
5. **Flooded bank after storm:** FLO-ECO-06 permits conditional transport only.  State/weather/hydrology must establish any debris, its identity, reachability, controller and quantity.
6. **Submerged plant with no flower:** existing pollination claim is inapplicable unless flowering/compatible pollinator facts exist; do not generate fruit.
