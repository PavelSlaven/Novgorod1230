## Method and current-pack comparison

Read-only library access: `root@192.168.1.100:/srv/library`; the INPX was searched locally on that host and each selected FB2 member was opened and parsed there. The current `production-v1/runtime-bundle.json` has 665 claims. I excluded its already-grounded broad premises: buoyancy, dry friction, contact geometry, stress/deformation, conduction gradient, phase-change energy, capillary/wetting, evaporation/humidity, iron moisture–oxygen corrosion, wood anisotropy/moisture/loading, metal state dependence, cold work/anneal/quench/temper, and fired-clay transformation.

The proposed refs below are deliberately topic-level (not an action whitelist). All are universal scientific candidates, `domain_internal_only`, high confidence/direct paraphrase unless a limit says otherwise. Exact values and outcome simulation remain code-owned.
