# Independent verification — pottery firing

**Scope:** independent re-opening of PFR-01 only.  This is a verification
verdict, not production authoring.  PFR-02–03 have no traced scholarly
provenance in the candidate file and are intentionally not imported.

## Source independently opened

- T. Neil Davis, [“Stoneware Pottery”](https://www.gi.alaska.edu/index.php/alaska-science-forum/stoneware-pottery-0), *Alaska Science Forum*, Geophysical Institute, University of Alaska Fairbanks, 7 December 1981.  Opened in full on 2026-09-04.  The paragraph beginning “After some hours of firing” says that, at approximately 1000 °C in the described firing, vitrification begins: chalklike clay fuses into a dense glassy material, aluminium-silicate crystals bind the matrix, and the body becomes more dense.  The surrounding text also makes composition, water removal, heating rate, quartz transition, cooling, and glaze conditions material/process-specific.

## Normalization constraint

If normalized, PFR-01 must remain a conditional transformation relation
(`clay body reaching vitrification` → `can become denser/cohesive by firing`),
not an unconditional `firing → ceramic` rule.  Its use is internal-only and
composes with separately grounded historical material/craft context; it does
not materialize that context.
