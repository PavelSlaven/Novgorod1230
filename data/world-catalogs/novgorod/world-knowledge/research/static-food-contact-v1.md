# Static food contact v1

One research candidate, outside production descriptor; independent verification pending.

Existing `claim:foundations-life-44-pathogen-transport` names broad possible transport modes, while `claim:heating-reduces-microbial-viability` covers heating. Neither states raw-animal-food transfer to cooked or ready-to-eat food through contact surfaces. `claim:foundations-earth-26-runoff-contaminant-transport` already covers sewage/runoff transport and is not duplicated.

FDA’s [Separating Food (Food Safety for Moms-to-Be)](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/separating-food-food-safety-moms-be), read September 4, 2026, defines cross-contamination as pathogen spread from foods, hands, utensils, or food-preparation surfaces to another food. Its same section identifies raw animal foods as possible pathogen sources, directs separation from ready-to-eat foods, and says bacteria from raw food can contaminate cooked food on an unwashed plate.

Candidate `claim:research-raw-animal-food-pathogens-can-transfer-to-other-food-via-contact-surfaces` therefore states only a conditional transfer mechanism. It establishes no actual food, contamination, cleanliness, dose, illness, safety, historical practice, or scene result.
