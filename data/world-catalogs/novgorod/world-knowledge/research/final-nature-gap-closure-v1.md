# Final nature/flora gap closure — candidate v1

This candidate adds 18 reusable qualitative premises for the static nature
closure. It does not create a tree, plant, bank, debris item, route, harvest,
food, animal, water condition, access, yield, identity, diagnosis, or outcome.

## Sources and narrow use

- [USGS: freeze–thaw and bank erosion](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/freeze-thaw-cycles-can-cause-bank-erosion): freezing water expansion and repeated thawing can break bank soil apart.
- [USDA ARS: freeze–thaw and soil detachment](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/project/?accnNo=419452): candidate uses only the qualitative relation between freeze–thaw, water state and detachability; it does not derive bearing capacity.
- [USGS: riparian vegetation and floodplain processes](https://pubs.usgs.gov/dds/dds-43/VOL_III/VIII_C05.PDF): vegetation/organic debris can slow floodwater, dissipate energy and permit deposition.
- [USGS: large woody debris](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2017/5120/sir20175120.pdf): channel transport and deposition of logs/branches.
- [USFS FEIS: American basswood](https://www.fs.usda.gov/database/feis/plants/tree/tilame/all.html), [spruce](https://www.fs.usda.gov/database/feis/plants/tree/picabi/all.html), and [alder](https://www.fs.usda.gov/database/feis/plants/tree/alnrug/all.html): bounded tree morphology, seasonal foliage and wet-site ecology. American basswood (*Tilia americana*) is used only as a genus-adjacent **analogical** bast-property comparator: it is not evidence for *Tilia cordata*, a Novgorod linden, local harvest, or medieval practice.
- [USDA NRCS: yellow water-lily](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_nulp.pdf) and [fragrant water-lily](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_nyod.pdf): rooted aquatic herbs may carry floating or emergent leaves.
- [USDA APHIS: poison hemlock assessment](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant_health/plant_pest_info/weeds/downloads/wra/Conium_maculatum.pdf): toxic look-alike risk; it does not place poison hemlock in Novgorod.

## Limits

The plant claims are deliberately anti-invention: a taxon, edible part,
preparation and current condition require their own support. Existing approved
named edible taxa remain distinct from this candidate. Modern general ecology
is used as universal qualitative science only; it is not historical evidence
for a particular thirteenth-century stand, fibre supply, shoreline, flood, or
practice.
