## Approved with limits

- PN-01, article 29: recognition of a lost/stolen thing is distinguished from
  tracing the acquisition chain (*svod*). A bare claim does not settle title.
- PN-02, article 32: the market-purchase clause names two free men or a
  *mytnik*; where the seller is unknown it supplies a witness/oath procedure.
  These are conditional contents of this legal text, not a universal witness
  rule or an automatically successful proof in the game.
- PN-03, article 45: witnesses are not required for the described deposit;
  a dispute claiming a larger quantity leads to the keeper's oath concerning
  what was deposited. This distinguishes custody, claimed amount and proof.
- PN-04, article 50: loss through sinking, war or fire is treated differently
  from dissipation of another's goods. The first branch permits repayment over
  time without coercion/sale; the latter leaves waiting or sale to the owners.
  Do not turn either branch into an automatic outcome for a concrete NPC.
