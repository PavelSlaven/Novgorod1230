# Residual legal primitives v1

## Scope

Candidate factual premises for a Novgorod-1230 legal/economic layer. They are documentary norms or dated records, never proof that a current scene has a court, claimant, offender, valid title, debt, witness, oath, jurisdiction, officeholder, sanction or remedy.

Primary text actually read: [Expanded Russkaya Pravda, Troitsky manuscript (second half XIV c.)](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/prp_t.htm), MSU electronic edition based on M. N. Tikhomirov (1953). Article numbers below are this exact version. The manuscript is later than 1230; use its text only as period-aware comparative legal vocabulary, not as automatically enacted Novgorod law. Dating/applicability control: Daniel H. Kaiser, [*The Laws of Rus’* introduction](https://faculty.washington.edu/dwaugh/rus/lexintro.html).

## Explicit exclusions

- Do not import Novgorod Judicial Charter, Pskov Judicial Charter, Sudebniks, or later named courts into 1230.
- No blanket claim that Russkaya Pravda was enacted uniformly in Novgorod, or that every article applied to every status, place or dispute.
- Existing corpus already covers individual debt, guarantee, property complaint, theft-inquiry and settlement-letter premises. New authoring should fill only a primitive absent from those records, with its specific article and dating caveat.
- Priority missing evidence: locally dated evidence for pledge/collateral, default, oath/witness use, and compensation in Novgorod practice around 1230.
