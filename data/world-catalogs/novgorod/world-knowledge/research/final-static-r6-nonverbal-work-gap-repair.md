# Final static R6: nonverbal coordination in shared risky work

Status: candidate editorial reconstruction. This note closes only the general
semantic gap for adapting communication while people coordinate an already
established risky shared task. It establishes no person, sensory condition,
relationship, work, load, tool, authority, skill, consent, cooperation, or
outcome.

## Reused approved premises

- `claim:military-world-message-needs-recipient-and-confirmation` supports the
  narrow general premise that a signal is clearer with observable content and
  acknowledgement; it does not prove authority, receipt, or compliance.
- `claim:public-world-route-and-tool-can-be-adapted-to-capacity` supports
  adapting an established limitation when means are available; it does not
  establish a limitation, means, companion, or result.
- `claim:human-life-category-does-not-determine-means-ties-skill` prevents age,
  gender, or status from determining skill, means, or willingness to help.

## Candidate reusable premises

- Before an already established shared task with a meaningful handling risk,
  participants can agree simple start, stop, or hold signals; this assigns no
  leader, exact signal system, task, load, agreement, or successful handling.
- A visual or tactile signal needs actual mutual perception and confirmation to
  function as shared communication; it is not automatically understood or
  received.
- Communication can be adapted to actual sensory capacity without inferring a
  person's inability, status, skill, or willingness to cooperate.

## Boundary

The candidate is a universal `social_behavior` coordination relation, not a
diagnosis, social script, authority rule, or work procedure. Existing state and
mechanics owners decide actual participants, perception, consent, means, task,
risk, capacity, handling, and outcome.
