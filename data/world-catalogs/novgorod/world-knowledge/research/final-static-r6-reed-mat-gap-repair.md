# Final static R6: reed mat preparation and interlacing

Status: candidate editorial reconstruction. This note closes only the semantic
gap between already committed cut reed stems and a possible floor-mat attempt.
It is not evidence that a reed bed, cutting tool, actor, usable stock, mat,
skill, access, quantity, workspace, or outcome exists.

## Reused approved premises

- `claim:common-reed-rhizome-produces-stems-in-wetland-habitat` concerns common
  reed and wetland/river-edge habitat; it does not create a reed source.
- `claim:reality-batch-01-wetland-reed-dry-intact-stems-can-be-distinguished-by-observation`
  and `claim:reality-batch-01-cut-reed-stems-can-be-gathered-before-moving-across-wet-ground`
  cover observation and gathering of already present cut stems.
- `claim:ordinary-life-weaver-needs-yarn-and-loom-space`,
  `claim:macro-b19-dried-willow-rods-can-be-moistened-for-flexible-basket-work`,
  and `claim:final-practical-wet-netting-and-cordage-can-need-inspection-drying-and-repair-before-reuse`
  support the narrow general premises that interlaced work needs suitable
  material condition and that drying/condition can matter. They do not state a
  reed-mat method.

## Candidate reusable premises

- Freshly cut stems can be sorted and left to dry or otherwise prepared before
  attempted mat work; this does not prescribe a sequence, duration, tool, or
  result.
- Prepared, sufficiently flexible continuous stems can be overlapped or
  interlaced and locally bound into a mat-like sheet; this is a qualitative
  construction possibility, not a recipe or a supplied binding material.
- A resulting mat's practical usability depends on actual stem condition,
  continuity, interlacing/binding, and drying; no load, durability, comfort,
  cleanliness, or successful floor use follows.

## Boundary

The candidate is a universal `physics_material_science` material-work relation,
not a local recipe. It must not create reeds, grant cutting or possession, set
an amount, provide cordage or tools, assign skill, or commit a mat. Any attempt
remains subject to existing material, access, quantity, time, and result owners.
