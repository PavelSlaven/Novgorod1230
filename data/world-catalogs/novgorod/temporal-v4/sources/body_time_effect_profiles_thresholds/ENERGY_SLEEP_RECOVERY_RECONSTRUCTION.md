# Energy sleep-recovery reconstruction v1

The canonical project source states that energy/wakefulness is restored by sleep or full rest but does not provide a rate. The approved reconstruction uses exact 25/9 points per hour while sleeping.

Derivation: ordinary awake expenditure is 25/18 points/hour. Sixteen ordinary awake hours spend 16 × 25/18 = 200/9 points. Eight sleep hours at 25/9 points/hour restore 8 × 25/9 = 200/9 points. Thus a regular 16-hour awake / 8-hour sleep day is energy-neutral before heavy-activity modifiers.

This is a gameplay balance reconstruction, not a medical or historical claim. Interrupted sleep keeps already committed recovery. Values clamp to 0..100.
