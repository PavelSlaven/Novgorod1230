## Result and remaining gap

No new production-ready mineral-substance relation is proposed here: every sufficiently anchored candidate above is already owned by a current fragment, or is universal physics rather than historical availability.  The real P1 gap remains a **reopened, material-specific Rus/Novgorod archaeological source** for lime mortar, glassworking, pigments, wax use, or resin production that states its object, date and process.  Do not promote MS-04–06 as historical availability on the cited general-science sources alone.

### New candidate disposition

MS-S7 yields one narrow new substantive historical relation (dated monumental mortar-analysis context). MS-S8–10 are research leads, not production candidates yet: a source title, table of contents, or library annotation cannot establish a workshop assemblage, glass process, or XII–XIII wax use. This distinction is intentional so that later authoring cannot silently turn bibliography into archaeology.
