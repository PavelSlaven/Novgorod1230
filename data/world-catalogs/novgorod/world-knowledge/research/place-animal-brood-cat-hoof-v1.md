## Candidate premises

- Ant workers tend immature stages; in ant species with silken cocoons, full-grown larvae make cocoons before pupation.
- In some ant species, workers carry eggs, larvae, or pupae to another nest site during colony budding. It does not explain an observed movement by heat, direction, disturbance, or current relocation.
- Cats can use quiet, secluded, or elevated hiding places. A missing cat cannot thereby be placed, diagnosed, or made responsive to a familiar cue.
- Equine feet bear weight, absorb shock, and provide traction. These functions do not diagnose an uneven stance.
- Basic hoof cleaning can remove rocks, dirt, manure, and other debris around the frog. It is not an instruction for forceful handling, shoeing, wound detection, or treatment.
