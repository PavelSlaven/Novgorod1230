# Final static streak R4.01: wheel fit and root-zone watering

Status: research note only. Modern general evidence, not a claim about Novgorod
in 1230. It does not create a particular wheel, plant, method, exact strength,
water volume, yield, price, diagnosis, or state transition.

## Drying wood and a metal-tired wooden wheel

Source: [USDA Forest Service, Forest Products Laboratory, *Wood Handbook*,
chapter 13, “Drying and Control of Moisture Content and Dimensional
Changes”](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/62200), “Dimensional
Changes” and “Moisture Content and Dimensional Change”. This government
technical handbook explains that wood changes dimension as moisture content
changes below fibre saturation and that in-service moisture changes can produce
dimensional change.

Candidate reusable premises:

- **RU:** При высыхании древесная деталь может изменить поперечный размер;
  у собранного деревянного колеса это может ослабить ранее плотную посадку
  обода или шины. **EN:** As a wooden part dries, its cross-grain dimensions
  can change; in an assembled wooden wheel that can reduce a formerly tight
  rim/tire fit.
- **RU:** Зазор, качание обода, выступившие стыки или движение шины — повод
  прекратить тяжёлую работу и осмотреть колесо; один внешний признак не
  доказывает единственную причину. **EN:** A gap, rim movement, opened joints,
  or a moving tire warrants inspection and avoiding heavy use; one visible sign
  does not establish a unique cause.
- **RU:** Осмотр должен различать как минимум влажность/усушку древесины,
  износ или деформацию шины, повреждение дерева и ослабленные соединения.
  **EN:** Inspection should distinguish wood moisture/shrinkage, tire wear or
  deformation, wood damage, and loosened joints.

Applicability: only when the committed object is a wooden wheel with a
committed rim or metal tire and visible condition supporting the observation.
This is a physical possibility, not a verdict that drying happened or that a
tire will fall off.

Exclusions: no asserted historical wheel design; no exact clearance, load,
speed, remaining distance, repair method, safety rating, or automatic failure.

## Moistening a plant's root zone under limited water

Source: [Colorado State University Extension, peer-reviewed “Healthy Roots and
Healthy Trees”](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/healthy-roots-and-healthy-trees/),
“Where Roots Grow”, “Why Roots Grow Where They Do”, and “Control and
Prevention”. It says roots require water and oxygen, saturated soil can kill
most roots, and long, deep watering over the whole root system with time for
soil to dry is better for trees than frequent light watering.

Candidate reusable premises:

- **RU:** Для уже укоренившегося древесного растения ограниченную воду имеет
  смысл направлять в доступную корневую зону и промачивать её, а не лишь
  многократно смачивать поверхность. **EN:** For an established woody plant,
  limited water is better directed through the accessible root zone and into
  the soil than repeatedly wetting only the surface.
- **RU:** Частое лёгкое смачивание, пересушка и длительное насыщение почвы
  водой — разные риски; корням нужны и вода, и кислород. **EN:** Repeated
  light wetting, drought, and prolonged soil saturation are distinct risks;
  roots require both water and oxygen.
- **RU:** Наблюдение за влажностью и уплотнением почвы рядом с корневой зоной
  помогает выбрать осторожное действие, но не доказывает состояние всех
  корней или будущий исход растения. **EN:** Checking moisture and compaction
  near the root zone can guide a cautious action, but does not prove the state
  of every root or the plant's future outcome.

Applicability: the deep-versus-light-watering comparison is limited to
established woody plants and usable soil around their root system. Soil texture,
drainage, plant type, season, rain, and committed plant condition remain
relevant.

Exclusions: no universal schedule; no exact volume, depth, timing, water
saving, growth, crop yield, diagnosis, or guaranteed recovery. Do not apply
the tree-specific recommendation unchanged to seedlings, pots, every
vegetable, waterlogged ground, or an inaccessible/absent water source.

## Gameplay/reconstruction boundary

These premises support a WK-only reconstruction such as “inspect the loose
wheel before loading the cart” or “use scarce water to wet the tree's root-zone
soil, then reassess it.” They do not materialize a wheel, water, tool, plant,
ownership, historical trade practice, or outcome. Any action still needs
committed access, source material, weather/place context, and its proper
code-owned owner.
