# Cordage reworking — gameplay reconstruction v1

Status: candidate rationale only. It creates no rope, fibre, tool, ownership,
skill, strength, fishing gear, catch, or successful action.

## Existing evidence

The approved `gameplay-natural-rope-v3.md` review of U.S. Army TM 3-34.86 and
UAF Sea Grant already establishes that fibre-rope condition, abrasion, bends,
knots and prior use matter to available strength. Existing historical claims
establish only that hemp cordage and cord in net-fishing contexts are compatible
with the setting; they do not establish any particular scene item or its make.

## Bounded reconstruction

Laid fibre cordage is assembled from component yarns or strands. If an already
present cord's construction, remaining length and condition permit, it can be
unlaid; recovered components can sometimes be retwisted or braided into a
thinner cord. Reworking does not restore lost length or strength and does not by
itself make a serviceable fishing line. Suitability still depends on the actual
material and condition, continuity, joins or attachments, and the load of the
intended use.

These are qualitative compositional premises for an ordinary physical object.
Code remains owner of identity, access, ownership, tools, mass conservation,
time, checks, exact load, breakage, persistence and action outcome. The claims
must not infer fibre material from an unidentified rope-like item or permit
altering somebody else's property.
