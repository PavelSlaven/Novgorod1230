# Reconstructed healer practice v1

## Ordinary healer practice

Status: editorial candidate under WK §0.2, authored by /root. This is a
plausible game reconstruction for Novgorod Land 1200–1300, not a historical
source, exact local medical institution or advice for real care.

Offline batch 01 case b01a-015 exposed an ordinary-role gap: the corpus
could describe illness support but not what a healer might actually ask,
carry, learn, earn or do with the household. Six compositional premises
address that role without enumerating illnesses or prescribing remedies.

The reconstruction composes existing approved learning/apprenticeship,
household means, travel/hospitality, religious practice and illness-support
premises. A sufferer's account, visible signs and ordinary observation
supply a plausible examination without diagnostic knowledge. Portable
cloth, a vessel, a bag and familiar dried plants supply a plausible limited
kit, rather than a modern doctor's bag. None identifies a plant or claims
that any remedy works. Teaching through experience, reputation-mediated
trust, negotiated compensation and household cooperation connect the role
to work and everyday life rather than assign every healer a fixed rank.

Prayer and bodily attention are allowed to coexist as an editorial cultural
possibility. A belief or reported recovery never establishes medical
efficacy. No treatment procedure, dose, disease diagnosis, germ-theory
knowledge, guaranteed recovery or modern professional credential is added.

The role may be settled or travelling, wealthy or poor, skilled or
unskilled; these are possible variants, not an excuse to omit the concrete
kit, work, learning and livelihood above. Current people, possession,
consent, illness severity, resources and outcomes remain scene/code-owned.

Independent review should check all six RU/EN premises for plausibility,
anachronism, usefulness, overstatement and these limits against the
committed candidate. Review must not call this note external attestation.
