**Scope:** independent check of `population-causal-materials.md`, not approval
of `population-environment-biology.md`. Verdicts are research dispositions;
none self-promotes a candidate into production.

## Method and source-opening record

- Re-opened cited source URLs through browser-harness. The USFS TreeSearch
  page for chapter 4 returned a service block, so its chapter identity was
  cross-checked against the USFS result for *Wood Handbook* chapter 4. The
  Illinois ceramic URL opened, but its visible page is generic “Scientific
  Principles”, not the claimed drying/initial-firing experiment text.
- The cited CCI iron URL returned 404. Independent CCI search results confirm

- CCI search results directly corroborated the leather directions: very low RH

- Verdict meanings: **APPROVE** = source/claim/qualifier fit; **REVISE** =

## Final source-path correction

The live CCI leather path was independently reopened after its stale URL was
found to return 404: [Care of Alum, Vegetable and Mineral-tanned
Leather](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/conservation-preservation-publications/canadian-conservation-institute-notes/care-alum-vegetable-mineral-leather.html),
“Relative Humidity and Temperature.” It explicitly says below 30% RH causes
moisture loss and embrittlement, while above 65% RH encourages mould growth and
hydrolytic degradation. The current source register now uses this URL. Thus
current claims 4 and 5 retain **APPROVE** with their existing conditional
limits; this re-opening does not supply historical-practice evidence.

The requested direct opening of the current USFS chapter-4 PDF
[`62243.pdf`](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/62243.pdf) was
blocked in browser-harness, but was independently reopened through the available
PDF reader. Its title page identifies Chapter 4. Page 4–3, lines 244–265,
defines water-vapour sorption and equilibrium moisture content; pages 4–9–4–10,
lines 971–1009, state that below fibre saturation wood shrinkage is largest
tangentially, intermediate radially and small longitudinally. Thus current
claims 1 and 2 are **APPROVE** again, retaining their species/temperature/
exposure and grain/magnitude limits. The prior temporary REVISE status records
only the browser-harness access failure, not a source mismatch.
