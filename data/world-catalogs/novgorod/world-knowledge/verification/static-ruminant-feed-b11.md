## Approved premises

- `claim:static-ruminant-feed-b11-legume-forage-can-support-frothy-rumen-bloat` — **APPROVE.** Merck directly describes stable foam trapping fermentation gas and identifies legume-dominant pasture, including clovers, as a common context. It does not diagnose every exposed or distended ruminant. [Merck Veterinary Manual](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/digestive-system/diseases-of-the-ruminant-forestomach/bloat-in-ruminants)
- `claim:static-ruminant-feed-b11-marked-distension-and-respiratory-distress-warrant-prompt-qualified-assessment` — **APPROVE.** Merck describes marked distension, dyspnea, and severe progression; the prompt-qualified-assessment boundary is a properly marked inference, not a diagnosis or treatment. The current Russian wording correctly says «выраженным затруднением дыхания». [Merck Veterinary Manual](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/digestive-system/diseases-of-the-ruminant-forestomach/bloat-in-ruminants)
- `claim:static-ruminant-feed-b11-some-feed-molds-can-produce-mycotoxins-with-animal-dependent-effects` — **APPROVE.** UMN states that certain grain molds produce mycotoxins and effects depend on toxin and animal factors. Its swine focus does not establish the same response in ruminants, horses, or poultry. [University of Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/swine/mycotoxins-harm-swine-health-and-reproductive-performance)
- `claim:static-ruminant-feed-b11-visible-mold-does-not-establish-mycotoxin-presence-or-level` — **APPROVE.** UMN states mycotoxins are not present in all moldy grain and laboratory analysis is needed for possible presence and level. Visible mold therefore supplies neither a toxin measurement nor a safety conclusion. [University of Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/swine/mycotoxins-harm-swine-health-and-reproductive-performance)

All four are modern veterinary/feed relations only: no medieval animal knowledge, local feed, diagnosis, procedure, dose, threshold, treatment, or outcome is established.
