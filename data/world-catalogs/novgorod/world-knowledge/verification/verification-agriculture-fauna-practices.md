# Independent verification — agriculture / fauna practices

**Scope:** independent, read-only review of AFP-01…07 from
`research/population-agriculture-fauna-practices.md`.  This is an approval
verdict for bounded historical compatibility only; it makes no production or
matrix change.

## Sources independently opened and checked

1. [КСИА 232 (2014), PDF](https://www.archaeolog.ru/media/ksia/ksia-232.pdf),
   printed p. 192.  The full PDF was opened and its p. 192 text was extracted
   and checked.  It says that the XI-century *Ustav* of Prince Yaroslav
   attests cultivation of hemp and flax, then states that fibre is obtained
   from a hemp stem and identifies tow as hemp-processing waste used when
   laying log structures and caulking gaps.
2. E. A. Rybina, [«Промыслы в средневековом Новгороде (по
   археологическим материалам)»](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/promysly-v-srednevekovom-novgorode-po-arheologicheskim-materialam),
   *Исторические исследования* 3 (2015), pp. 219–234.  The full publisher
   text was opened.  The relevant anchors are p. 229 (hunting as an economic
   activity), p. 230 (XI tribute and XII–XIII birch-bark animal references),
   p. 233 (the complete ash hunting bow; Nerev hooks for climbing trees for
   honey), and pp. 233–234 (written honey and wax evidence).

| Candidate | Verdict | Independent finding and mandatory limits |

It does not establish a 1230 Novgorod field, seed, crop stage, grower, calendar, yield, or harvest. |
| AFP-02 | **APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS** | The same page explicitly says: «Из стеблей конопли добывали пеньку».  Hemp-stem → fibre is a supported material/process premise; its medieval-Rus contextual application is **inferred/medium**, not evidence of a present plant or processed fibre.  It says nothing about flax processing, quantities, quality, tools, or successful work. |

Approve the two narrow relations as **inferred/medium** broad 1100–1300 compatibility.  No tar, waterproofing guarantee, existing log structure, prepared tow, labour, tool, or construction outcome follows. |

This is **direct/high** dated Novgorod attestation for the form/category.  It neither materializes a bow nor establishes arrow possession, hunter, prey, access, range, performance, damage, or a successful hunt. |

Approve **direct/medium** only as a regional economic-context synthesis.  It is not evidence that any particular resident hunted, that game/fur exists at a scene, or that an actor has rights, skill, equipment, tax duty, or a hunting result.  The XI evidence gives continuity context; it is not itself a 1230 event. |

It must not become default urban scenery, a present bee colony/tree cavity/honey/hook, an urban household activity, a skill, or a domestic hive. |

It does not establish an apiary or hive form, bee biology, harvest method, a current product/quantity, food safety, trade rate, or ownership.  The p. 233 mention of a robbed **пасека** belongs to a first-quarter-XIV document and cannot be used to populate 1230 household hives.

## Boundary verdict

All seven candidates are admissible only with the limits above.  AFP-01–03
are historical-continuity/material-process premises, not local 1230 finds.
AFP-04 is the only directly dated second-quarter-XIII find.  AFP-05 is a
regional economic synthesis rather than individual causality.  AFP-06–07
mean **бортничество**—tree-based/rural wild-bee practice in the author's
description—not a generic domestic hive or a household default.  None of the
claims proves a scene, actor, resource, permission, stock, yield, or outcome.
