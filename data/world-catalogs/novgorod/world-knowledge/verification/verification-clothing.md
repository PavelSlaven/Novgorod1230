# Independent verification: clothing material and construction

Root independently opened M. G. Rabinovich's chapter, *Old Russian clothing
IX–XIII centuries*, in *Ancient clothing of peoples of Eastern Europe* (1986,
pp. 40–111): https://www.booksite.ru/ancient/reader/human_3_02.htm . Checked the
material, undergarment, outerwear and opening headwear sections, separately
from the researcher. The web page also contains later-period chapters: those
are not evidence for this approval.

## Verdicts

- CL18–20: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. Wool, flax and hemp textiles; homespun cloth
  for ordinary clothing; plain and twill linen weaving are explicitly described.
  These are broad Old-Rus compatibility premises, not a Novgorod stock in 1230.
- CL21–24: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. Shirt as a main garment; linen/fine wool and
  richer silk examples; folded-panel tunic construction, hem gores and underarm
  gussets. Do not make this one reconstruction a mandatory garment pattern.
- CL25: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. Cast bronze and bone collar buttons are stated;
  wood is explicitly probable. Keep that distinction or omit wooden buttons.
- CL26: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. A male shirt can be belted by a narrow leather
  belt with metal fittings or a woven cord. No compulsory outfit follows.
- CL27: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The author's model distinguishes quantity and
  variety of clothing/materials more than cut across social strata. This does
  not infer any NPC's wealth, status, personality or actual possessions.
- Additional approved premises from the same checked section: narrow trousers
  with gusset and drawstring (Levasheva's reconstruction); onuchi as cloth
  strips wrapped around foot/shin; sheepskin winter kozhukh worn fur inward;
  svita as an outer garment (the exact cut is uncertain); sleeveless votola
  cloak (a hood is only possible, not established).
- CL01–03/05/08: omit research-method/scope metafacts from this gameplay pack.
- CL09–10/33: later excavated headwear does not establish a 1230 item. No
  positive headwear claim approved from these later-find candidates.
- Follow-up: independently read the complete headwear subsection. APPROVE
  WITH LIMITS for a pointed cap depicted on a gusli player on a XII-century
  bracelet, and towel-like povoi headcover wrapped over the hair. These are
  separately supported period forms, not the rejected XIV–XV finds. No
  mandatory outfit, status inference or legal sanction is imported.
- CL28–32: not reviewed in this pass; no production approval implied.
