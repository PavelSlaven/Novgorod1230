## Evidence limits and missing premises

- **Cough and vomiting:** direct NLM sources now cover voluntary-versus-reflex cough and reflex emesis.  A candidate still must remain qualitative.
- **Sneeze:** direct scientific support is now in the supplement below; it must remain a nasal-irritant reflex, not an effect of every irritant.
- **Choking and drowning:** direct official NHS and WHO definitions now close the previous source gaps.
- **Shock:** label is clinically overloaded. Current bounded candidate is blood-volume → pressure/perfusion → compensation.
- No source establishes mandatory cough, fall, drop, flee, freeze, vomit, or unconsciousness.
- Universal physiology creates no injury, water, alcohol, exposure, actor knowledge, or medieval practice.

## Unseen composition probes

1. Carrier steps on hidden sharp object while holding vessel: possible withdrawal competes with load, footing, and body state; no automatic drop/wound.
2. Dust, smoke, insect, or food particle irritates airway: protective response may be possible; clearance/choking/harm remain conditional.
3. Real submersion with respiratory impairment: oxygen/consciousness risk without drowning timer or assumed aspiration.
4. Committed blood loss over time: mechanics own quantity/time; perfusion premise prevents unlimited-performance narration.
5. Abrupt rise after committed fluid/blood deficit: possible temporary balance/awareness impairment, not guaranteed faint/fall.
6. Bright light, bang, or lost footing: blink/startle/postural correction may occur; perception/action remain state-specific.
7. Cold wind after wet clothing: existing heat exchange plus shivering supports narration; no fixed temperature/hypothermia.
8. Drink with committed ethanol content/consumption: body owner may apply impairment; WK creates neither drink nor dose/tolerance/outcome.
