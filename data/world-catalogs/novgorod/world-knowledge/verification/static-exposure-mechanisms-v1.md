## `claim:research-carbon-monoxide-is-colorless-and-odorless`

**APPROVE.** CPSC's [carbon-monoxide guide](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/pdfs/blk_media_coguide.pdf) states that carbon monoxide in its pure form is colorless and odorless. RU/EN wording adds no source, concentration, exposure, detector, symptom, diagnosis, safety, or scene result.

## `claim:research-local-exhaust-can-remove-contaminated-air-from-a-room`

**APPROVE.** CDC/NIOSH's [Control of Formaldehyde during Embalming Procedures](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/engcontrols/ecd/detail17.html) describes local exhaust hoods capturing formaldehyde with an exhaust fan outside the embalming room. RU/EN wording remains a conditional airflow mechanism, not proof of a working vent, draught, contaminant, concentration, safe level, or scene result.

## `claim:research-cold-water-immersion-can-impair-muscle-control`

**APPROVE.** National Weather Service [Cold Water Hazards and Safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/coldwater) identifies physical incapacitation as loss of muscular control and places it in cold-water immersion. RU/EN wording does not establish temperature, duration, person, impairment degree, diagnosis, injury, survival, treatment, or scene result.

## `claim:research-fire-plume-can-transfer-combustion-products-to-an-upper-layer`

**APPROVE.** NIST's [CFAST Technical Reference Guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/firemodels/cfast/master/Manuals/CFAST_Tech_Ref/CFAST_Tech_Ref.tex) directly states that a fire plume transfers entrained mass and heat from the lower to upper layer; its Species Transport section states that products generated by fire are carried in the flow and accumulate in layers. RU/EN wording preserves the conditional lower-to-upper transport relation. It establishes no actual fire, enclosure, opening geometry, smoke quantity, layer height, temperature, concentration, visibility, exposure, safe location, or scene outcome.
