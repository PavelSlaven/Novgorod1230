# Social and daily-practice candidates: Novgorod, c. 1230

**Status:** research only; no candidate is self-approved or production-ready.
This is a role/context and ordinary-exchange substrate, not a generator of
NPCs, offices, occupations, money, goods, rights, schedules or transactions.

## Independently opened academic source

- **SDP-S1 — E. A. Rybina, “Промыслы в средневековом Новгороде (по
  археологическим материалам),” *Исторические исследования* 3 (2015),
  [full publisher-hosted text](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/promysly-v-srednevekovom-novgorode-po-arheologicheskim-materialam).**
  Read in full.  Relevant printed pages: p. 219 (agriculture, craft and trade
  alongside fishing, hunting and wild-bee keeping in the economic context),
  pp. 220–221 (fish, money and grain in tribute/documentary context), p. 230
  (XIII-century sale of beavers for grivnas), pp. 233–234 (honey/wax and
  documentary debt/tribute contexts).  The article is an archaeological and
  documentary synthesis, rather than one isolated letter.

Existing compiled 420 claims already cover debt-record, grain-procurement,
hired-work, accounting-unit, priest-address, Christian-text and calendar-text
relations.  They are not repeated below.

## Deliberately unresolved

- **Religious labour/routine:** the compiled corpus already has a priest’s
  address and Christian textual/calendar relations.  Neither SDP-S1 nor the
  checked records establishes a general clerical work routine, parish office,
  church employment, attendance requirement or authority in Novgorod 1230.
  No candidate is made for that gap.
- **Service roles:** a reference to a collector, messenger, hired worker or
  priest is not a general occupational taxonomy.  No modern “service job,”
  guild, wage schedule or stable NPC role follows.
- **Measures and money:** SDP-03 demonstrates an accounting amount in one sale;
  it does not establish a coin, exact mass, exchange rate, price list or a
  universal commercial measurement system.

## Scope boundary

These rows are factual context for compositional retrieval.  Exact parties,
consent, ownership, access, transport, inventory, measure conversion, balance,
debt validity, religious office and routine timing remain committed state,
authority or mechanics.  They must not be inferred from an actor’s description
or from the fact that the source records a historical practice.
