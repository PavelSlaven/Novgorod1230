**Status:** candidate editorial reconstruction only. It is not production,

## Method and boundaries

This candidate addresses three confirmed ordinary-composition gaps from the
final partial-family audit, then adds bounded religious and institutional
context requested for the same final pass. It uses the WK §0.2
`editorial_reconstruction` route: qualitative possibilities for an already
established context, not a claim that the context is present or that a result
follows.

Existing checked records inform the boundary but are not silently relabelled
as direct evidence for new claims: Russkaya Pravda property/market/entrusted
goods relations; birch-bark documentary records; `social-institutions.json`;
the verified Olenin/Yaroslav religious-role records; existing water/exposure,
memory, public-dispute and ordinary-lifeways claims. Every new claim instead
uses the candidate editorial source and must be judged on plausibility,
scope and localization by an independent reviewer.

## Possession and return inquiry

Holding a thing, keeping it for another, claiming it, and having a settled
right to retain it are not interchangeable. A found object leaves its earlier
connection unresolved; transfer context, custody, markings, messages and
witness accounts can direct a return/provenance inquiry without settling title,
theft, abandonment, duty or outcome.

## Household water and waste

Ordinary water use needs an already available access point, vessel and labour;
carrying, cooking, washing and cleaning compete for those means. Washing may
change visible soil but not prove cleanliness or safety. Where space and means
allow, separating waste from water collection, food handling and frequent
passage limits direct contact; drainage, weather and later handling can alter
conditions. No well, latrine, water, contamination, illness, installation or
cleaning result is created.

## Observation, retelling and corroboration

Position, light, noise, duration, obstruction and attention bound what can be
noticed. Retelling may distinguish or blur direct observation, hearsay,
inference and later memory. Independent accounts, messages and traces may be
compared, but agreement does not prove truth and disagreement does not settle
credibility. This is epistemic context, never an admissibility rule or a
verdict about an NPC's intent.
