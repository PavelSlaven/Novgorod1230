# ADR-009: Physical NPC schedule in the live CDDA path

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-21
- Decision scope: physical NPC schedule and the boundary of temporary model-selected intents to approach or retreat from the hero in the live CDDA path of the vertical slice; ADR-003 remains accepted for spatial-v3.

## Context

The CDDA reuse-first rule in `AGENTS.md` and owner decisions D266/D329 select native CDDA mechanics for Part 2: D266 sets the residents’ schedule with native hunger and sleep, and D329 requires real log-to-plank work and native CDDA responses governed by code without a model. The approved Part 2 plan assembles the schedule from `work_hours`, `guard_pos`, an hourly recurrent EOC, native ground travel, sleep and hunger. Owner decisions D338 and D339 add the native faction camp: the yard, its territory and its items belong to the household camp, its residents use them freely, and the household food is available to them even when they start hungry. Owner decision D266 approves the authored schedule data for the slice. This ADR records the owner boundary; implementation and activation are established by the slice code, mod data and tests.

Owner decisions D352/D353 assign ordinary responses to the hero to code using context, mod-owned weights and short phrases, and seeded CDDA randomness; the NPC model is called only on escalation. D355 makes this response rule normative before implementation and gives a model-selected temporary intent priority over schedule transitions. D359 requires danger and strong needs to interrupt the intent as native CDDA interrupts an ordered position. In native CDDA, the order remains set and execution resumes afterwards. These decisions narrow the model movement prohibition below without transferring physical execution or schedule ownership to the model.

## Decision

In the live CDDA path, CDDA owns physical execution of an NPC's schedule: place by game hour, travel to it, guard post, sleep, hunger and food, flight from fire and return to the post. The slice mod supplies the approved NPC class, work-hours window, posts and hourly recurrent EOC, and marks the yard as a camp of the owner faction through the native `camp` field of the overmap special. A member of the owner faction placed by mapgen inside that camp becomes its resident through native `basecamp::add_assignee` and `NPC_MISSION_CAMP_RESIDENT`. Outside an active temporary movement intent, on shift the NPC keeps its post; off shift the native resident branches apply — `camp_work` only within the class work hours, `free_time` and `return_to_camp` — and food and drink come through native `address_needs`. CDDA's existing movement, sleep, need and camp mechanics perform the behavior; no second schedule or movement engine is introduced.

When the native path has no schedule data, the NPC retains CDDA default behavior. Missing authored data required by an approved scenario is caught by mod acceptance tests, not by a runtime `npc_schedule_gap` hard block. The approved-source requirement in the temporal contract remains in force for authored data.

The NPC model cannot propose a physical schedule transition, directly move the NPC, assign coordinates, or change its permanent post or mission in this path. It may select a temporary intent to approach or retreat from the hero through `request_movement` with `movement_intent: "approach" | "retreat"`; CDDA executes it through the native `goto_to_this_pos` order and `goto_ordered_position` behavior goal. The hero is the target actor, and mod data supplies the completion distance separately for each intent; the model selects neither a number nor a reference to a distance. Part 2 removes the JS `request_movement` → `npc_move` route; this permission requires native execution and does not reinstate direct movement through that route. Semantic NPC decisions, conversation, speech and knowledge remain governed by the active NPC contracts, including [the autonomous contract §7.7, §8.3 and §19.3](../../data/knowledge-source/corpus/DOCUMENTS/npc_autonomous_decision_contract.md).

A temporary intent ends only when its semantic distance goal is reached, a new model decision after a new escalation replaces it, or CDDA cannot construct a path. Reaching an intermediate path cell does not complete the intent. Changes to `work_hours` and the hourly yard routine EOC skip their schedule transition while the intent is active, without cancelling the intent or queuing the skipped transition; after completion, the NPC follows the schedule for the current time. Danger and strong needs temporarily take over execution without clearing the order, and CDDA resumes the intent afterwards. The intent itself changes neither `guard_pos` nor the NPC's mission. The native order lacks an EOC setter, save/load persistence and a moving target; the autonomous contract requires those capabilities without prescribing their implementation. These requirements take effect before implementation and do not assert that the missing capabilities are already present.

## Boundary with ADR-003

ADR-003 continues to assign pure schedule and perception proposals to `@rus/npc-runtime` in spatial-v3; the temporal contract continues to assign traversal there to `@rus/movement-routes`. This ADR defines physical schedule execution and the boundary of temporary model-selected intents to approach or retreat from the hero in the live CDDA path. It does not cancel ADR-003 or change the spatial-v3 path, which is removed by a separate task.
