# Residual language and education pass v1

## Scope

Static candidate research for four cartography families: historical spoken language and register; names, patronymics, address and titles; everyday literacy, writing and accounting; education, apprenticeship and skill transmission. It adds no scene actor, current document, literacy rate, school, apprenticeship, office, relationship, or outcome.

## Source

- A. A. Zaliznyak, ["Общие сведения о берестяных грамотах"](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/about-source/general-information), scholarly excerpt from *Древненовгородский диалект*, second edition (2004), with 2017 supplement, published by HSE and the Institute of Slavic Studies RAS. Relevant sections: `Внешний вид, нумерация`; `Датирование`; `Содержание, специфические трудности изучения`.

The source covers the surviving XI–XV-century corpus. It is therefore a Novgorod corpus envelope, not direct proof of a particular condition in 1230. Claims retain this range instead of treating earlier or later material as an exact-1230 observation.

## Novelty check

Existing v5 claims already cover Old Russian predominance and the general proximity of household letters to living speech. Existing records cover individual named addresses, particular instructional texts, and museum context for Onfim. This shard does not repeat those claims: it adds the register distinction, corpus text/material categories, register function range, name-label uncertainty, and instructional-type set.

## Source-search limits

The consulted scholarly corpus overview directly supports instructional text types, but does not support an institutional school, a named teacher/pupil relation, craft apprenticeship, observation/correction practice, or a general patronymic/title system. No candidate claims assert those missing systems. Direct, date- and location-bounded evidence would still be required to turn those residuals into factual gameplay premises.
