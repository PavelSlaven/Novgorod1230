# Final practical gap closure — candidate rationale

Status: candidate only. This note does not activate a claim, establish a scene, or provide operational medical, fire, or repair instruction.

## Editorial boundaries

The arms, wet-use and storage claims are deliberately compositional ordinary-world premises. They apply only after the equipment, material, birth, exposure, store, net, hull, or fire is independently established. They create no possession, authority, combat statistic, diagnosis, available helper, tool, stock, weather, access, schedule, repair, survival, or outcome.

Weapons and protective equipment are treated as carried physical objects: retention, access, fit, clearance, visible condition and conditional maintenance matter; no claim identifies a weapon class, wearer, skill, protection value, damage, or combat result. Wet nets, cordage and wooden hulls similarly gain only inspection/drying/conditional-care context, never a vessel, leak, fibre, craft procedure or safe passage. Storage claims distinguish cautious observation and possible separation/air movement from proof of spoilage or contamination.

## Health and exposure

[WHO postnatal care recommendations](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241506649) support continued attention to mother and newborn after birth. Candidate claims retain only observation, warmth, feeding, available support and a bounded escalation cue; they do not diagnose haemorrhage, infection, feeding success or any complication.

[CDC chemical-emergency guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/chemical-emergencies/response/index.html) supports separation from an uncertain source and communication of observed exposure details when help exists. The candidate therefore preserves source/route/timing observations instead of naming poison, dose, infection or cure. The parasite premise is editorial and remains only hygiene, observation and contextual help seeking.

## Storage, wet work and fire

The storage envelope is a conservative extension of existing grain-moisture and airflow claims: moisture, odour and visible change justify cautious checking, while separation and air movement are conditional means rather than guaranteed preservation.

[USFA fire-extinguisher guidance](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/fire-extinguishers/) distinguishes ordinary combustible, flammable-liquid, electrical, metal and cooking-oil fires and stresses a clear escape route. Together with the existing NIST Class-A water-cooling claim, this supports a narrow fuel-class limit: water cooling may concern ordinary solid combustibles, not an unclassified, electrical, liquid or oil fire. It makes no promise of suppression and introduces no numerical, steam, scald or firefighting procedure claim.
