## Explicit non-claims / authoring boundary

- HA-01–06 and HA-16–17 overlap approved production records and should be reused rather
  than re-authored.
- HA-07–08, HA-13–19 and HA-31–32 are broad category/context facts: they do
  not license a scene inventory, worker or status.
- HA-20–26 use earlier local strata as compatibility evidence; production
  wording must retain inferred/medium temporal applicability around 1230.
- HA-27–28 record commodity dealings, not crop yield, stored amount, price
  mechanics or automatic access. HA-29–30 are rejected: a record of rye or
  barley cannot define a universal grain-procurement input list.
