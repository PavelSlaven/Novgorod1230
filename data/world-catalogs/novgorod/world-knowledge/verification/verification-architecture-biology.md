# Verification & approval audit: architecture-settlement and biology-physiology

**Role:** independent verification of foreign research shards. This file does not alter their candidates, activate a profile, create a location/NPC, or supply exact body mechanics.

## Method and verdicts

- Each cited landing page was re-opened with browser-harness on 2026-09-03. A rendered, accessible page is not itself a page-level archaeological or clinical anchor; verdicts therefore retain the source's temporal and evidential scope.
- `APPROVE` and `APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS` are the only production-eligible verdicts. “Production-eligible” means eligible to be compiled as a qualified factual claim, **not** evidence of current scene presence, mechanics, or an authored fact.
- `NEEDS_EVIDENCE` marks a missing, broken, too-broad, or non-verifiable anchor. Its corrected wording is a safe boundary only; it must not be promoted as an affirmative historical/scientific claim.
