# Cognitive and social foundations — library research candidates

**Status:** research only; no production, source approval or historical bridge.
These are modern, qualified premises for a person or group already present in
committed state. They never create a person, relationship, memory, goal,
emotion, norm, competence, institution, consent, promise, social rank or
outcome; none is an NPC option list or a diagnosis.

## Corpus check

The current runtime bundle (665-claim corpus at task start) already has body
limits, sleep loss, injury, animal sociality and dated social/legal records.
It does **not** supply a general human framework for selective attention,
memory limits, learning/skill acquisition, attribution, attitude--behavior
conditions, group influence, affiliation/ostracism, prosocial decision or
contextual social roles/norms. The candidates below do not duplicate a named
historical occupation, legal duty, animal behavior, or a code-owned NPC state.

  scores, lie detection, personality typologies and psychodynamic explanation:
  excluded as unsafe, over-specific or not needed for world mechanics.

  effect sizes and treatment advice: not sourced as reusable premises.
- Trust calibration, reciprocity, deception detection, bargaining, apology and
  repair could use a newer experimental social-psychology source; this batch
  only provides the narrower attribution/persuasion/conflict substrate.
- Culture-specific norms and real historical institutions remain historical
  research, not imports from modern general psychology/sociology.

experimental social-psychology/behavioural-science source, retaining explicit
conditions and population-level language.

### Result and non-promotion boundaries

MS3 supplies 10 narrow candidates across the requested axes. It treats fear/threat,
frustration, stress, trust repair, reciprocity and bargaining as conditional
experimental mechanisms. It deliberately does not add a generic “fear causes X”,
"stress causes risk-taking", “apology restores trust”, or “unfair offers must be
rejected” rule. Independent review should separately decide whether these
laboratory/synthesis findings are appropriate world-knowledge primitives.
