# Independent verification — animal husbandry candidates

**Scope:** independent, read-only verification of AHP-01 and AHP-02 in
`research/population-animal-husbandry.md`. No production or matrix change.

## Source independently read

M. Hamilton-Dyer, «Палеозоологические материалы и динамика промысловой
фауны», in N. A. Makarov (ed.), *Археология севернорусской деревни X–XIII
веков*, vol. 3 (Nauka, 2009), printed pp. 16–24, local
`.tmp-pr92-sources/northern-village-3.pdf`; official source URL:
https://www.archaeolog.ru/media/series/arch-northrus-village/Arch_northrus_village-3.pdf.

I independently inspected the rendered printed pp. 16–24, especially p. 23.
The chapter concerns Minino by Lake Kubenskoye, northern Rus, over X–XIII
centuries; it is not a direct Novgorod-city or 1230 household record.

| Candidate | Verdict | Exact source result and production-safe wording | Limits / novelty check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AHP-01 | **REJECT_DUPLICATE** | Printed p. 23 says the role of domestic animals in Minino inhabitants' economy increased in the Middle Ages, while wild-animal remains still predominate. It does **not** yield the submitted universal-looking `animal husbandry → meat provisioning` relation. | The submitted relation is an unspecific meat-provision premise—the class explicitly excluded from this pass—and adds nothing beyond existing managed-domestic-animal / food evidence. The source has no dated Novgorod-1230 meat operation, animal, herd, carcass or supply. Do not promote. |
| AHP-02 | **APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS** | Printed p. 23, paragraph beginning «Разделка туш домашнего скота…»: domestic-animal carcasses were processed within a settlement, making entry of their remains into the cultural layer more likely. **Approved narrow wording:** **Разделка туши домашнего животного в пределах поселения → может оставлять → костные остатки в культурном слое. / Processing a domestic-animal carcass within a settlement → can leave → bone remains in its cultural layer.** Direct/high for the Minino taphonomic interpretation. | New only as a qualified deposition/process relation; it is **not** animal-existence, meat provision, a slaughter rule or free bone source. For Novgorod Land 1100–1300 it is broad **inferred/medium** compatibility. No current carcass, slaughter, meat, bones, quantity, processing location, tool, worker, ownership, food safety or disposal practice follows. |

## Decision

One of two candidates is eligible. AHP-02 can enrich a conditional
carcass-processing/waste query only after an independently grounded carcass
and place exist; it must not materialize either. AHP-01 remains rejected.
