# Interior conditions P1 closure review

## Probe

An actor moves a damp fibre item or perishable from a drying area into an enclosed store. The question is whether a new factual relation is required to reason qualitatively about moisture and preservation.

## Existing composition is sufficient

No new candidate is needed. The loaded pack already supplies the causal relations that are general enough to use once authoritative state supplies the item and actual conditions:

- `claim:food-drying-depends-on-humidity-airflow` — humidity and airflow affect drying; it gives neither weather nor a completed result.
- `claim:population-physics-evaporation-humidity` — evaporative water loss depends on temperature and surrounding humidity; it gives no drying time or food-safety result.
- `claim:drying-reduces-microbial-growth-conditions` — drying reduces available water to microbes, while moisture returning can permit regrowth.
- `claim:microbial-growth-depends-on-available-moisture` and `claim:microbial-growth-depends-on-species-temperature-range` — moisture and organism-specific temperature range constrain microbial growth.
- `claim:food-drying-reduces-microbial-growth-conditions` — food drying reduces microbial-growth conditions without proving safety or stock.
- `claim:stored-grain-condition-depends-on-temperature-moisture` — a storage-specific confirmation that temperature and moisture constrain stored grain; no stock or duration follows.
- `claim:population-material-wood-moisture` and `claim:material-water-new-linen-water` cover their named material limits, but do not turn arbitrary fibre into linen or provide a storage outcome.

The staged grain claims add historical/process context only; they do not create an additional preservation or room-climate premise.

## Boundary

The facts do **not** support the missing-looking shortcut “enclosed store ⇒ stagnant/humid air ⇒ spoilage”, nor “drying area ⇒ adequate airflow”, nor a room inventory or a materialized ventilation feature. Enclosure, ventilation, humidity, temperature, item wetness, microorganism, duration, placement and current room function are separate scene/state or code/time-owner inputs. Condensation likewise needs an actual temperature, vapour and surface-state basis; `claim:foundations-earth2-08-rising-air-cooling-cloud-condensation` is not a room-condensation shortcut.

Therefore the semantic result may state only a conditional: if the committed environment keeps the item sufficiently moist and otherwise supplies the microbial-growth conditions, preservation risk can increase; if it supports drying, the inverse constraint applies. It must not assert that either condition exists in this store or resolve an item’s condition, spoilage, durability, or elapsed time.

## Disposition

`P1 interior function + storage ventilation/condensation/spoilage` is closed for the stated open-action probe by composition, with the boundary above. No new production fragment, source, room-function claim, materializer assertion, descriptor change, approval or promotion is warranted.
