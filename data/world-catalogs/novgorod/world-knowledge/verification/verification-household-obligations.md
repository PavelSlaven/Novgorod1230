# Independent verification — household obligations

**Scope:** HHO-01–03 only.  This verifies passages in the Extended *Russkaya
Pravda* as a limited normative-text envelope; it does not create an enacted
1230 Novgorod law, household, kinship, duty, property, or remedy.

## Sources independently opened

1. Moscow State University, [*Prostrannaya Russkaya
   Pravda*](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/prp_t.htm), Troitsky copy of
   the second half of the fourteenth century.  Full text and §§93, 95–96 were
   opened on 2026-09-04.  §93 gives the small-children/nearest-relative rule,
   return of principal goods, and the holder’s gain because they fed and cared
   for the children.  §96 names the person with whom the mother lived and who
   fed her in the stated no-`язык` succession case.
2. M. N. Tikhomirov, [“Spiski i proiskhozhdenie redaktsii Russkoy
   Pravdy”](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/intro.htm), MSU scholarly
   edition, pp. 16–19 and §3, pp. 23–25.  Opened in full at the relevant pages.  It states that
   the oldest extant Kormchaya copy with the text was written around 1282 in
   Novgorod, the Troitsky copy itself is from the second half of the fourteenth
   century, and the Sinodal/Troitsky common prototype arose before the last
   quarter of the thirteenth century, probably in Novgorod.

## Dating and normalization boundary

All three are direct passages of the edition. Tikhomirov’s §3 (pp. 23–25)
argues that the Extended Recension was composed as a single edited monument and
states his view that it arose in Novgorod at the beginning of the thirteenth
century, connecting it with the 1209 uprising. He also records serious
criticism of that Novgorod-origin hypothesis and concludes only that it retains
the right to exist. That supports the same **1200–1300, Novgorod-land,
inferred/medium, unknown-typicality** comparative textual envelope already
used for the project’s other Extended-Pravda records.

This is not an undifferentiated law, direct enactment at Novgorod in 1230, or
player-facing legal knowledge. If authoring follows, retain
`domain_internal_only`, an explicitly conditional textual/normative literal,
and no claim that a specified household, actor, property, duty, or remedy is
present or enforceable.
