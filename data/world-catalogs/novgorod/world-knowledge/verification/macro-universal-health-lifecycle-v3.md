## Approved

### `claim:candidate-v3-child-development-has-recognizable-periods`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus divides child growth and development into infancy, preschool years, middle childhood years, and adolescence. The claim does not infer a particular child's age or development.

### `claim:candidate-v3-puberty-can-coincide-with-final-growth-spurt`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus states that a final growth spurt begins at the start of puberty. The claim retains that exact temporal relation without a personal age, timing, or growth rate.

### `claim:candidate-v3-aging-changes-cell-function`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus states that cells change with aging and that many lose functional ability or function abnormally. The claim is limited to possible reduced functional capacity.

### `claim:candidate-v3-aging-organs-gradually-lose-function`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus states that aging organs slowly lose function and that reserve loss varies between people and organs. The claim preserves both qualification and no individual outcome.

### `claim:candidate-v3-pressure-beyond-bone-capacity-can-fracture-it`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus states that a bone will split or break when more pressure is put on it than it can stand. The corrected claim now retains pressure rather than broadening it to force.

### `claim:candidate-v3-dislocation-disrupts-normal-joint-position`

**APPROVE.** MedlinePlus defines a dislocation as disruption of the normal position of bone ends where they meet at a joint. The candidate does not infer a concrete injury or treatment.

### `claim:candidate-v3-oral-bacteria-acid-can-damage-enamel`

**APPROVE.** NIDCR states that tooth decay begins when oral bacteria make acids that attack enamel and can lead to a cavity. The candidate preserves only the initial relation.

### `claim:candidate-v3-disability-reflects-health-and-context-interaction`

**APPROVE.** WHO states that disability results from interaction between health conditions and environmental and personal factors. The candidate stays at that general relation and does not attribute disability or an outcome to any person.

All eight claims in the corrected candidate are approved. The unverified poison-route claim was removed rather than carried forward.
