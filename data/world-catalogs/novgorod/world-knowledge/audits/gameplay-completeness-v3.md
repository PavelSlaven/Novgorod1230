## Boundary

Static category coverage answers only whether current approved claims have
meaningful families and honest applicability. It cannot prove that arbitrary
free actions resolve correctly, that retrieval returns the needed premise, that
runtime delivers it to presentation, or that the entire historical world is
covered. Do not claim gameplay saturation or full world coverage from this
audit.
