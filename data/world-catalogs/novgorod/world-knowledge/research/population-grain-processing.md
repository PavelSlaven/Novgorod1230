## Hansson: transferable process facts

The source concerns Swedish prehistoric/early-medieval bread, not Novgorod.
Thus these are general food-process relations only; they do not assert a
Swedish ingredient, bread type, grave practice, oven or inventory in Novgorod.

observations and are explicitly held from production.

## Existing verified boundary

The current pack already has separately grounded, non-duplicated anchors for:

- Rurikovo Gorodishche bread ovens → oven bread baking (IX–X / early-XI
  context only);
- Novgorod letter 974 → malt commodity/payment context, not brewing;
- letters/household records for rye and barley commodity context;
- Desyatinny scythe and ploughshare → grass cutting and tillage compatibility.

The local caution supplied for this task is retained: a Filipova 1997 Novgorod
mill reference dates the earliest Novgorod millstones to the XIV century. It
cannot ground a 1220–1240 local mill or handquern claim.

## Held relations

`grain → handquern → flour`, `flour → sieving`, `malt → brewing`, and
`grass/hay → winter fodder` remain un-authored. They need a correctly resolved
full primary/scholarly source plus a stated scope; generic food knowledge and
later Novgorod millstones are insufficient.
