# Foundations: mind and society — independent source/domain verification

**Scope:** independent review of the 40 MS research candidates in
`research/population-foundations-mind-society-library.md`.  This is not an
approval of production records, a historical bridge, an NPC policy, or an
assertion about any actor's current mind, relationship, knowledge, norm,
status, consent, or future conduct.

## Sources actually checked

I opened the listed FB2 members read-only from the library server:

- Гиппенрейтер, `f.fb2-173909-177717.zip!/175037.fb2`: lectures 5, 7, 8,
  10, 14--16.  The text itself labels the action/goal/operation material as
  activity theory; its statements must not become a motive or goal detector.
- Лурия, `f.fb2-193389-195730.zip!/194855.fb2`: Part II, *Восприятие*,
  *Внимание*, *Память*.  The checked passages state the competing-stimulus,
  novelty, task/set, tactile, rhythmic-duration and recognition relations.
- Майерс, `f.fb2-190928-193388.zip!/191935.fb2`: chapters 3--4, 6--8,
  11--13.  The attribution chapter explicitly distinguishes situational and
  dispositional attributions and documents their fallibility.  The book is a
  social-psychology textbook: group and persuasion results remain conditional,
  not predictions for a particular person or culture.
- Кравченко, Анурин, `f.fb2-238404-242733.zip!/238631.fb2`: chapters 3, 6,
  7 and 9.  These are sociological concepts and typologies; they cannot
  establish a real local norm, authority, role, sanction, membership, or duty.

## Domain decision

Do **not** use `biology_physiology` or the historically contextual
`social_law_economy` as catch-alls.  If a later approved corpus and consumer
create the proposed domains, individual perception, learning, attribution and
affect belong in `psychology_behavior`; interpersonal/group/role/norm concepts
belong in `social_behavior`.  All eligible claims are literal `supported_fact`,
universal, `domain_internal_only`; they supply bounded semantic context only.
They never materialize a mental or social state.

## Appendix B — independent MS2/MS3 source review

**Scope:** review only.  I re-opened the MS2 FB2 passages read-only and read
the full public research pages for MS3.  The experimental results below stay
inside their task, stimulus, sample and group-result boundaries.  They do not
create an actor's emotion, memory, trust, relationship, stress state, intent,
consent, bargain, or future conduct.

### Sources actually checked

- Гиппенрейтер, `f.fb2-173909-177717.zip!/175037.fb2`, lecture 5: the text
  calls nonconscious mechanisms technical executors of conscious action and
  says many arise when conscious functions are transferred to a nonconscious
  level; lecture 10 covers sensory corrections in formation of motor skill.
- Майерс, `f.fb2-190928-193388.zip!/191935.fb2`, ch. 13: in the social-dilemma
  discussion, those who do not expect cooperation also refuse it to protect
  themselves from exploitation, and noncooperation can feed distrust.
- Berdica et al. (2018), *Threat vs. Threat*, abstract/results: free-viewing
  eye tracking found stronger initial capture by unpleasant than neutral
  pictures, but no general sustained-attention preference.
- Miendlarzewska et al. (2013), *Working Memory Load*, abstract: a two-load
  N-back/threat-of-shock experiment found high load at exposure attenuated
  immediate recognition enhancement for negative distractors.
- Dixon et al. (2024), *Using Wordle*, abstract: Wordle feedback indicating
  approach versus thwarting produced group differences in self-reported
  affect, motivation and frustration.
- van Herk et al. (2024), *Heightened SAM- and HPA-axis activity*, abstract
  and methods: systematic review of 44 eligible studies, with decision-related
  cognitive, motivational, affective and predictability domains.
- Ma et al. (2019), *Apologies Repair Trust*, abstract: N=221 Chinese
  undergraduates in a two-round trust game; apology after zero return increased
  re-investment relative to violation without apology; perceived
  trustworthiness mediated it and negative emotion moderated that path.
- Horita (2020), *Greater effects of mutual cooperation and defection*,
  abstract/results: direct-reciprocity iterated-prisoner's-dilemma experiment;
  prior mutual cooperation/defection respectively increased/decreased later
  cooperation.  This is the actual paper at the listed PLOS URL, not the
  research table's misattributed “Hayashi et al.” citation.
- Vavra, Chang & Sanfey (2018), *Expectations in the Ultimatum Game*,
  abstract/methods: explicitly supplied offer-distribution histograms changed
  acceptance/rejection behavior for a subset of participants.
