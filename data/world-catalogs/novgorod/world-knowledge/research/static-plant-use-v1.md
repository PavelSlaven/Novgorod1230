# Static wild-plant use v1

**Status:** research-only candidate set, pending independent review. It is outside
the production descriptor and does not establish Novgorod-1230 presence, a
specimen, identification, access, inventory, safe consumption, preparation,
exposure, or outcome.

## Reconciliation

- Existing `claim:candidate-edible-plant-status-does-not-transfer-to-every-part`
  sets the general part-bound limit; it does not name usable parts of a taxon.
- Existing `claim:bearberry-lingonberry-resemblance-not-identification` means a
  lingonberry food-part fact cannot identify a concrete berry.
- Existing `claim:mezereon-poisonous-sap-all-parts` already supplies the named
  toxic-property premise, so it is not duplicated.
- Existing `claim:candidate-nettle-bast-fibre-retting-decortication` covers
  nettle fibre only, not food parts or contact properties.

## Proposed bounded premises

| Taxon | Relation | Source and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Urtica dioica* | Young tender leaves are documented edible food parts. | Oregon State University Extension, [Wild Edibles: Stinging Nettle](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/em-9373-wild-edibles-stinging-nettle), opening. The same source lists multiple parts, but this candidate keeps the useful leaf relation only. No identification, present plant, amount, preparation, safety or outcome. |
| *Urtica dioica* | Trichomes can produce a skin sting or welts. | Same OSU source, “Gathering nettles carefully and respectfully.” A contact property only; no actual contact, injury severity, allergy, treatment or outcome. |
| *Vaccinium vitis-idaea* | The fruit is documented edible. | Oregon State University, [Landscape Plants — *Vaccinium vitis-idaea*](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/vaccinium-vitis-idaea), plant description. No identity from resemblance, present berry, ripeness, amount, safety or outcome. |
| *Corylus avellana* | Hazelnuts are documented edible. | NC State Extension [Filbert — *Corylus avellana*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/corylus-avellana/common-name/filbert/), “Edibility.” No present shrub, identification, ripeness, harvest, amount, safety or outcome. |
| *Rubus idaeus* | Fruit can be eaten raw or cooked. | NC State Extension [*Rubus idaeus*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rubus-idaeus/), “Edibility.” No present plant, identification, ripeness, harvest, amount, safety or outcome. |
| *Malus sylvestris* | Fruit is documented edible, while seeds are poisonous. | NC State Extension [Apple — *Malus sylvestris*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/malus-sylvestris/common-name/apple/), “Edibility” and “Fruit Description.” Part-specific only; no tree or fruit, identification, amount, exposure, symptom, treatment or outcome. |

This is deliberately not a recipe, toxicity catalogue, treatment guide, or a
claim that every plant part or visually similar specimen is food.
