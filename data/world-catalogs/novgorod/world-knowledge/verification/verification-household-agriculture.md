- HA-07–08: APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS. The Novgorod Museum footwear article explicitly

## Casting supplement

Root also independently read the Desyatinny report's XII-century casting
paragraph and MIA 32 p. 165. HA-C01–06 APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS after correcting
the proposed predicates: crucibles, pouring ladles and moulds are **attested
uses**, not mandatory tools for every possible casting process. Copper,
bronze, brass, tin, lead and gold are separate attested materials, not one
required alloy. Sprues and defective castings are documented process traces.
The studied XII-century firesteel has an iron base with a welded steel
striking portion; this is an attested composite form, not all firesteels.
No current workshop, stock, output, recipe or numerical alloy specification
follows. Use broad 1100–1300 inferred/medium medieval compatibility and keep
archaeological technical detail domain-internal.
