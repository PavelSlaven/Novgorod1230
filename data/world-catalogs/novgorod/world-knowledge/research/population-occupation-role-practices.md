# Occupation role practices — bounded historical research

**Status:** research candidates only.  This is neither an approval nor a
production record.  It does not establish a present NPC, officeholder,
military unit, command, duty, access, equipment, route, conflict, or result.

## Existing coverage deliberately not duplicated

The current corpus already has separate, narrower evidence for a hired worker
in a business record, a hired boatman, a letter addressed through authority,
the conditional merchant-loss rule of *Russkaya Pravda*, and smith/carpenter
contexts.  This pass therefore does not restate any generic hired, merchant,
craft, or authority role.  It looks only for a dated military-office event.

## Independent source read

[*Новгородская первая летопись старшего и младшего изводов*](http://litopys.org.ua/novglet/novg23.htm),
Moscow–Leningrad, 1950, pp. 261–275, electronic presentation of the critical
edition.  The full 1220–1229 section was opened through browser-harness on
2026-09-04.  Its entries for 1224 and 1228 expressly name a `посадник` and
describe the respective events.  This is a chronicle record of particular
actors and episodes, not an occupational manual or a general institutional
rule.

## Composition boundary

If independently approved, these are historical event-context facts only.
They can support a qualified answer that named officeholders could take part in
the recorded military episodes.  They cannot turn a current character with a
title into an attested person, supply a force or boat, or infer a mandatory
practice, authority, or outcome.
