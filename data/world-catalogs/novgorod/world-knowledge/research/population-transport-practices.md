# Transport practices — bounded research candidates

## Scope and duplicate boundary

Research only.  No production/matrix recommendation follows. Existing pack
already covers wooden boat/cart/sledge forms, cart street use, oars, boat
repair, joinery, caulking and horse transport value. Do not duplicate those
object or construction relations. The former candidate relations concerned
transport use, portage and supporting work; the source correction below
withdraws them.

## Source-attribution correction

The earlier version incorrectly attributed the transport passage to A. V.
Andrienko, pp. 202–239, in the 2019 IIMK volume. Reopening the cited volume
shows that article concerns walrus ivory, not the asserted transport
passages. TP-01–TP-05 are therefore withdrawn. The phrases first seen in an
aggregated search snippet have no reopened, attributable academic source and
page anchor; they must not be reused.

One genuinely identified publication remains only as duplicate control:

A. M. Grinev and V. K. Singh, **«Деревянная деталь повозки XI в. из
   раскопок в Великом Новгороде»**, *Краткие сообщения Института
   археологии* 275 (2024), pp. 391–405,
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.25681/IARAS.0130-2620.275.391-405).
   The Troitsky find is an early-XI-century cart turning mechanism/front fork;
   authors reconstruct a four-wheeled vehicle and note restricted mobility in
   narrow urban streets. It is useful control evidence, but not a promotion
   candidate because cart existence/use is already compiled.
