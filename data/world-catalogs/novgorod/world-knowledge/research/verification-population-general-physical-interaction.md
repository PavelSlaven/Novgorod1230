# Independent verification — general physical interaction

**Scope:** independent verification of the 14 current candidates in
`population-general-physical-interaction.md`. These are universal qualitative
physics facts, not evidence that a historical tool/material exists or that an
actor knows scientific terminology. They do not create items, tools, loads,
heat, water, a successful outcome, or a recipe/action whitelist.

## Independently opened publisher sources

All seven current OpenStax College Physics 2e pages were opened directly.

| Source | Direct supporting anchor |
| --- | --- |
| P1 [Elasticity: Stress and Strain](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/5-3-elasticity-stress-and-strain) | Opening discussion; tension/compression: force changes shape, small elastic deformation returns, larger stress permanently deforms/fractures; change in length varies with material, force, original length and cross-section. |
| P2 [Pressure](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/11-3-pressure) | Definition `P = F/A`; same force over a smaller contact area gives larger pressure. |
| P3 [Friction](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/5-1-friction) | Opening/static and kinetic-friction discussion: friction opposes relative or attempted motion; normal load and contacting substance matter. |
| P4 [Archimedes’ Principle](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/11-7-archimedes-principle) | Opening/definition: every immersed object has upward buoyancy, equal to displaced-fluid weight; float/sink depends on buoyancy relative to weight. |
| P5 [Phase Change and Latent Heat](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/14-3-phase-change-and-latent-heat) | Opening phase-change explanation: melting/vaporization need energy; freezing/condensation release it; phase change can occur without concurrent temperature rise. |
| P6 [Conduction](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/14-5-conduction) | Opening conduction discussion: net heat flux follows temperature difference; rate varies with material, area and thickness. |
| P7 [Humidity, Evaporation, and Boiling](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/13-6-humidity-evaporation-and-boiling) | Opening humidity/evaporation discussion: humidity inhibits evaporation, hotter air increases evaporation rate, and vapor can enter gas phase below ordinary boiling point. |

## Candidate-by-candidate verdict

| ID | Verdict | Verified safe wording and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPI-01 | APPROVE | A force/load can deform a solid. Deformation remains material-, geometry- and loading-direction-dependent. |
| GPI-02 | APPROVE | A sufficiently small elastic deformation can reverse after load removal; it is not a property of every bend/object. |
| GPI-03 | APPROVE | Greater stress can cause permanent deformation or fracture; there is no universal breaking threshold. |
| GPI-04 | APPROVE | Tension/compression deformation of a rod varies with material, applied force, original length and cross-section. |
| GPI-05 | APPROVE | For the same perpendicular force, smaller contact area means greater pressure. This does not guarantee penetration. |
| GPI-06 | APPROVE | Static friction opposes attempted sliding up to a contact/load-dependent limit; it does not certify a knot, grip or footing. |
| GPI-07 | APPROVE | Sliding friction depends on the contacting materials and normal load; no universal wetness/lubrication coefficient follows. |
| GPI-08 | APPROVE | An immersed object experiences upward buoyancy equal to the weight of displaced fluid, including when it sinks. |
| GPI-09 | APPROVE | Whether an object floats depends on its weight relative to buoyancy/displaced volume; shape may matter through displaced volume. A material label alone proves no capacity. |
| GPI-10 | APPROVE | Melting and vaporization require energy input; energy may be absorbed during a phase change without simultaneous temperature increase. |
| GPI-11 | APPROVE | Freezing and condensation release thermal energy; actual phase conditions remain necessary. |
| GPI-12 | APPROVE | Conduction transfers heat through material down a temperature difference; rate depends on conductivity and geometry, with no numerical temperature inferred. |
| GPI-13 | APPROVE | Evaporation can occur below ordinary boiling temperature; a hearth is not a physical prerequisite for evaporation. |
| GPI-14 | APPROVE | Evaporative water loss/rate varies with temperature and surrounding water-vapour conditions; it establishes no fixed drying time, safety result or facility. |

## Result

**14 APPROVE, 0 REVISE.** The claims are source-faithful qualitative
relations. They are suitable only for objective semantic reasoning with actual
material, geometry, load, heat, placement and time supplied elsewhere. Actor
facing text must remain ordinary-observable rather than grant modern scientific
knowledge; historical availability and actor-specific skill require separate
evidence and access gating.
