### `claim:place-some-ant-workers-can-carry-immature-brood-during-colony-relocation` — APPROVE

pupae to another nest site during budding. Limits are essential: new nest site

### `claim:place-equine-feet-bear-weight-absorb-shock-and-provide-traction` — APPROVE

horse's stance, pain, injury or hoof condition, or prescribe trimming, shoeing
or treatment.

shoeing, farriery procedure or treatment.
