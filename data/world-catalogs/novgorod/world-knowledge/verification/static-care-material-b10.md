## Approved premises

- `claim:static-parchment-or-vellum-should-not-be-wetted` — **APPROVE.** CCI directly supports cockling and distortion from wetting parchment/vellum, with irreversible gelatinization limited to degraded parchment. It is a material-risk limit, not a document diagnosis or restoration result. [CCI](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/conservation-preservation-publications/canadian-conservation-institute-notes/basic-care-books.html)
- `claim:static-fragile-parchment-or-inked-record-needs-conservative-specialist-treatment` — **APPROVE.** NPS specifically places parchment, vellum and iron-gall ink among materials needing professional expertise for controlled humidification and flattening. It supplies no procedure, ink composition, damage diagnosis or recovery guarantee. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/conserve-o-gram-13-2-flatten-folded-rolled-paper.htm)
- `claim:static-minor-thermal-burn-first-aid-is-cool-water-and-no-adherent-removal-or-grease` — **APPROVE.** NHS directly supports prompt cool running-water cooling, leaving adherent material in place, and avoiding creams, oils and butter. This neither diagnoses a concrete burn nor says when a person can resume work. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds/)
- `claim:static-burn-severity-or-location-red-flags-need-urgent-qualified-care` — **APPROVE.** NHS lists the stated size, depth, location, chemical and electrical red flags. It is an escalation boundary, not a severity classifier or outcome prediction. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds/)

The burn premises are modern first-aid/triage context, not evidence of a Novgorod-1230 medical institution, practitioner, practice or treatment availability. A real conflict would arise only if a consumer presented modern qualified care as an available historical service; this candidate does not do so.
