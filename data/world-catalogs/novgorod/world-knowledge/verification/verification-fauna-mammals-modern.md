# Independent verification — modern mammal biology

**Scope.** Independent source check of FMM-01–FMM-43 in
`population-fauna-mammals-modern.md`; no production approval or edit.  The
eleven cited Animal Diversity Web accounts were independently opened via the
authorized web fallback on 2026-09-04.  ADW is published by the University of
Michigan Museum of Zoology / Ecology & Evolutionary Biology. Its accounts are
usable as an educational scientific source for these qualitative,
species-conditional relations, not for a historical-Novgorod occurrence.

Every approved item below is admissible only as `biology_physiology`,
`supported_fact`, `context_scope: universal`, `knowledge_access:
domain_internal_only`. It never creates an individual, present population,
den, nest, cache, route, food, actor skill, ownership, access, catch, hunting
outcome, combat value, exact time/date, or 1230 presence.

## Source and normalization boundary

The verification accepts the cited ADW text as a qualitative scientific basis,
while retaining its stated variation by habitat, food availability, population,
weather, sex, age, and individual condition. It does not approve numerical
values, month calendars, food recipes, modern range/distribution, conservation,
or management content.

FMM-23, FMM-29, FMM-31 and FMM-41 must be split before any authoring. All
other FMM-01–22, FMM-24–28, FMM-30, FMM-32–40 and FMM-42–43 are
**APPROVE_WITH_LIMITS** using the candidate relation text and the limits in
this report.
