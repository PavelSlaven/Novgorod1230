# Independent verification — fibre preparation and dyeing

**Scope:** FPD-01–03 from `research/population-fibre-preparation-dyeing.md`.
This checks a northern-Rus archaeological technical substrate only. It neither
asserts a Novgorod-1230 workshop, crop, worker, tool, yarn, textile, dye-bath
or garment, nor approves a dyeing claim.

## Source independently opened

A. V. Kudryashov, [«Деревянные орудия и приспособления для обработки
прядильных растений с поселения Луковец (X–XII вв.)»](https://bibliotekar.ru/rusNovgorod/164.htm),
*Новгородский музейный вестник* 21 (2007), full article, opened 2026-09-04.
The article identifies Lukovets at the Sheksna; building horizons in excavations
1–3, 5–6 run from the mid-tenth to the last quarter of the twelfth century,
and excavation 4 from the late tenth to the beginning of the thirteenth, dated
by dendrochronology and radiocarbon analysis. It explicitly says the wooden
tools concern mainly flax and hemp.

## Normalization boundary

FPD-01 and FPD-02 are usable only as conditional historical tool-function
relations; FPD-03 is a narrowly dated site-context relation rather than a
general craft chain. Keep all three `domain_internal_only`, typicality
`attested`, confidence `medium`, directness `inferred`, and applicability
1100–1300 / `region_novgorod_land` only if the runtime text names the earlier
northern-Rus source and says compatibility is inferred. No dye-material,
mordant, dye-bath or dyeing-operation relation was verified here.
