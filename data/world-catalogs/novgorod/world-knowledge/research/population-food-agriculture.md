# Food and agriculture process candidates

**Status:** author research only; no production authoring. Relations are
conditional compatibility, never a 1230 inventory, yield, meal, farm or NPC
occupation.

## Independently opened sources

- IIMK RAS, [Rurikovo Gorodishche field report](https://www.archeo.ru/field-research/annotations/raskopki-myisovoj-chasti-novgorodskgo-ryurik-12aee), opened 2026-09-04. It records eight open bread ovens in ditch slopes and dates their construction to the IX–X turn / ditch fill to end XI; it also describes a production zone. It supports historical baking facilities, not a c.1230 oven.
- Birch-bark corpus, [Novgorod letter 974](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/974/), opened 2026-09-04. It is a fragmentary business/debt record, conventionally dated 1180–1200 with movement earlier; extrastatigraphic range late XII–first quarter XIII. Existing editorial reading identifies payment concerning malt. It is not a beer recipe or brewing event.
- IA RAS, [Desyatinny excavation](https://archaeolog.ru/press/articles/n167), previously opened in current research set: ploughshare with early field; later garden/orchard layer includes scythes. Plot was abandoned by early XIII, excluding 1230 presence.

## Assessment

Actual accessible sources confirm the already-authored bread-oven, malt,
grain, tillage and scythe anchors, but do not yet justify new grain-milling,
brewing, preservation or fodder prerequisites. Keeping these as explicit holds
avoids converting general knowledge into a historical Novgorod mechanism.
