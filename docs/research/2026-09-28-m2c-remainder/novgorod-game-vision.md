Owner (PavelSlaven) corrected and confirmed Claude's understanding on 2026-09-25:

- Player may make anything realistic, **including weapons and forged documents** — never easy: needs resources, skills and technology. Only unique quest items are a typed gap. Everything else is limited only by realism and resource availability.
- The game's distinctive idea: we do NOT enumerate all things/mechanics. Code defines **all possible categories/subcategories** of things; the **LLM creates concrete instances** by choosing category/subcategory; **code assigns characteristics** (LLM may propose them to code). Following a fixed procedure.
- **World Knowledge Platform is central**: gameplay runs on a weak local LLM (fits 24 GB VRAM, qwen 27B), so WK exists to reduce hallucinations/inventions in almost any game situation. Always mention/use WK when discussing materialization or LLM decisions.
- **LLM may create NPCs too**, bounded by preset limits. Field split (owner, 2026-09-25, #133): code sets the NPC's **name (from an approved regional name pool — never the LLM: weak model gives anachronisms/wrong-ethnicity/real historical figures)**, role, occupation, legal status, appearance, items, routine, attributes, origin, **and (owner 2026-09-26, D1) temperament, values, 1–3 goals/fears, motives** from approved occupation/role profiles at creation (D-006 unchanged); the LLM writes only speech manner and a short bio retelling code facts at the player's first interaction, rejected by code if it invents kin/offices/historical persons. LLM «creates» an NPC only by mapping the player's request to a role; code creates it if the persisted presence roll said present and the limit allows (D8). Player character: name/character/goal from the player (D11). Places have schedules (night watch, day market) — realised through NPC routines (`@rus/npc-runtime`, ADR-003) and place access (`@rus/turn`+`@rus/party-store`, ADR-004), not through presence rolls (D5). Presence is rolled at first **arrival** (not first «осмотреться»), per season period for seasonal categories (D3, D6). Weather owner stays `@rus/environment-state` (D7). All in #133 comment 5839745154.
- **Items have owners who can recognize them (owner D14, 2026-09-26, #133 5844152943):** code sets the owner at creation (household, master, trader, church, authority; wild finds ownerless or «lost by someone» without an invented person), `@rus/items-property` keeps ownership; code assigns distinguishing marks (brand, pattern, repairs, wear — never LLM-written, D9); owner and people who know the item keep that knowledge in `@rus/visibility-knowledge-memory`; code decides «recognized / not» on perception; NPC reaction via npc decisions, legal consequences (theft, returning) via `@rus/social-law`. Item truth (authenticity, legality): marks in items-property, evaluation in social-law (D10 clarified).
- Code also creates concrete instances: on materialization it fills the place (NPCs, items, environment) AND sets **per-category limits** (people, item categories) so the player can't conjure endlessly, AND a **presence probability per category** for that place. A limit ≠ guaranteed presence: people in a deep forest or a sword found there are unlikely but possible; a gold cup on a mountain top — improbable, not impossible. Presence is a chance, decided once and kept (no reroll by rephrasing/reload).
- Skill/effort affect only **finding** what exists, not presence. Presence probabilities and limits are authoring data approved by the senior model.
- **Anti-abuse:** "find a thing" materialization per place is finite: (1) first arrival + look — one discovery pass; (2) first opening of each container — may hold a surprise; then nothing more. Repeated looking only re-finds what already exists, even if the category limit isn't exhausted. New things appear only through real world events/time, not through repeated searching.
- **Digging/probing hidden spots is not a surprise container.** A buried/hidden man-made thing needs a causal reason to be there (authored cache, event, someone hid it). Digging yields only natural resources of the place (worms in season, stones, clay…) from its finite stocks — otherwise the player would wander and dig endlessly.
- **Roles of the two knowledge stores (owner, 2026-09-25):**
  - **World Knowledge** = model of the world (physics, chemistry, biology/medicine, craft procedures, law, social norms of the epoch). Giga retrieves relevant context for the gameplay LLM so it knows how a procedure goes and doesn't hallucinate: forging a sword → tools, temperature, technique (LLM sees if the player's attempt is nonsense); belly wound → consequences/risks; crime witnessed by a fisherman and a druzhinnik → legal consequences and reactions of the epoch.
  - **`novgorod_world` / `novgorod_world_v17`** = everything implementable by code + the categories the LLM works with during materialization. Must be **as full as possible**.
- **WK decisions after the WK audit (owner, 2026-09-26, #133 5846022014 / 5846025182):** D15 open WK to NPCs — `npc_decision` gets all WK domains as world constraints like the player turn step; `conversation` (NPC speech) gets only what a person of 1230 could know (law, custom, craft/daily practice reclassified actor-visible), never modern science explanations; access change = new pack revision + approval. D16 WK wired to narrator (`narration` profiles) and player-conversation interpreter. D17 reranker (bge-reranker-v2-m3 candidate) moved from M3 into current work, pinned by profile like Giga. D18 **no spoilers from the future**: everything time-dependent (events, who is alive/in office, prices, famine) is checked against the party's current in-game date; not-yet-happened facts never reach the LLM in any form (WK slice, narration, NPC knowledge, identifying text). Audit numbers: of 386 needed facts only 5% delivered fully, 20% partly; 32% missing from pack, 24% retrieval miss, 17% planner miss, 3% access; false "supported" in 35% of situations; planner said "no knowledge needed" in 30% of player actions (report: session scratchpad wk-audit/report/report.md).

- **Who may know what (owner, 2026-09-26, later stage):** knowledge differs by social group; different groups tell different rumors. Not in the current WK CRs, but they must keep the existing extension points: WK actor facets / `role_bound` claims (`knowledge_access.required_values`, today only 15 claims for `nov_role_fisher`) and `@rus/visibility-knowledge-memory` as the owner of who knows what. D15 reclassification must not flatten everything to «everyone knows».
- **The game will expand to the whole world; Novgorod land is only the start.** Hence two layers in world_base: universal (world-wide) categories/templates + regional permissions (`region_category_options` with weight/commonness, region_* profiles). Non-Novgorod entries (deserts, oases, paddies…) are normal, not noise. Contract: `world_base_materialization_table_requirements.md` §4–7; G0 = historical-geographic region.
- Result: a freely materializable world bounded by historicity and realism.
- Narrator writes prose **like a book, through the player character's eyes, no inventions**. Thin prose = world-filling problem upstream. Current Codex prose output is "мусор".
