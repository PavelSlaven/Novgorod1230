## Verdicts

| Claim group | Verdict | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starch gelatinization | APPROVE | UMD Introduction directly states heated aqueous starch swells into a viscous gelatinous mixture used to thicken broth. No recipe or result. |
| Vegetable fermentation | APPROVE | VCE directly treats salt, oxygen exclusion/submersion and temperature as conditions affecting fermentation. No safety or batch verdict. |
| Fish heating | APPROVE | UF/IFAS support is direct for the stated protein/water/texture mechanism; texture is not a safety test. |
| Fish gutting | APPROVE | FAO §3.3.7 directly supports viscera/blood removal and gall-bladder caution. |
| Exposed fish drying | APPROVE | FAO Dried fish directly supplies sun/air moisture removal plus weather and pest/dirt exposure; this narrows, rather than duplicates, generic drying. |
| Fish salting | APPROVE | FAO Salted fish directly supplies opposite water/salt movement and dependence on distribution, concentration, time and drying. |
| Cooling/freezing | APPROVE | MSU says refrigeration slows rather than stops bacteria and freezing does not kill bacteria. The claim rightly makes no safety guarantee. |
| Rodent attraction/exclusion | APPROVE | CDC directly states food, water and nesting attraction, and recommends sealed food/openings; candidate says only “can reduce,” not perfect exclusion. |
| Honey-bee forage/surplus | APPROVE | OSU directly states suitable light/temperature prerequisites and that colony/forage conditions determine foraging capacity. It adds conditions beyond existing daylight-only honeybee coverage. |

All eleven claims are direct/high, universal, `domain_internal_only`, and
meaningfully narrower than the existing broad food, drying, fermentation and
honeybee premises. No claim turns appearance or texture into safety.
