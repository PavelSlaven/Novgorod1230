# Residual government and law research v1

Scope: static factual envelope for Novgorod close to 1230. This note does not model a state machine, present office-holders, a court, ownership, sanctions, borders, or mechanics.

## What remains intentionally unclaimed

- No solid primary attestation was found in the 1220–1240 chronicle entries for a standardized **sotnia** civic unit. It is not default local government for 1230.
- The inspected evidence names individual ends but does not date a five-end arrangement, much less establish that every end had its own assembly or territory. It cannot repair the undated v9 five-ends claim.
- No Yanin/Gippius work with a directly inspectable passage establishing early-1230 office competences was found in this pass. Those competences therefore remain unclaimed rather than reconstructed from later Novgorod institutions.
- *Russkaia Pravda* is not used as an operative Novgorod court code. Its layered text and later manuscript survival make it unsuitable here for a generic crime → proof → authority → remedy flow.
- Source records permit factual historical texture, not creation of a present prince, veche, court, office, territorial authority, crime, victim, defendant, sentence, or external relation. Those require scene-authoritative inputs and code-owned state/mechanics.
