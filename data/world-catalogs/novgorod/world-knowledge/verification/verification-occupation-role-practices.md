## Non-transferable limits

Neither event establishes a standing military or boating practice, duty,
power, chain of command, crew, boat, weapon, tactic, permission, route, or
present conflict. Do not materialize a named person or infer another
officeholder's conduct from either entry.

## Role-practice readiness

These can support only source-qualified narration of two named late-1220s
episodes. They **do not close** a general `occupation → practice` gap: two
officeholders acting during conflict are insufficient evidence that all
posadniks routinely commanded, fought, pursued by boat, or were obliged to do
so. A generic role-practice relation needs a source that actually states such
a role relation.
