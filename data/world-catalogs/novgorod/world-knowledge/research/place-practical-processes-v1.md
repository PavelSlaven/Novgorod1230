# Place practical processes v1

## Scope

Three bounded place-first gaps: hoop fit on staved vessels, clarification of visibly muddy water, and wick-fed lamps. This note does not prescribe procedures, temperature, safety, or success.

Authoring: `/root/place03_practical_processes`. Root integration: `/root`.

## Sources and limits

- U.S. Department of Labor, [DOT cooper / barrel raiser](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/oalj/PUBLIC/DOT/REFERENCES/DOT07E): full indexed occupation-page text read; describes compressing staves to loosen hoops and tightening replacement hoops. Modern occupational description; it does not document a Novgorod tool set or guarantee a leak repair.
- U.S. EPA, [Water on Tap](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1008ZP0.TXT): full indexed NEPIS text read; distinguishes settling, filtration, and a separate disinfection step. Modern municipal explanation, not a historical household method or safe-water result.
- U.S. EPA, [Industrial Wastewater Treatment Technology Database](https://watersgeo.epa.gov/iwtt/treatment-technologies): official indexed excerpt read for the `Cloth Filtration` entry, which says water passes through cloth media to remove solids. No full database export, household-cloth specification, or removal rate was read.
- U.S. EPA, [Total Solids](https://archive.epa.gov/water/archive/web/html/vms58.html): full indexed page text read; distinguishes dissolved solids that pass through a filter from suspended solids. It is not a complete contaminant inventory or a safety assessment.
- Rutgers University, [Surface Area Effects as Related to Flammability](https://www.chem.rutgers.edu/cldf-demos/1558-surface-area-effects-as-related-to-flammability): indexed explanatory excerpt read; it describes liquid wax rising through a wick by capillary action before combustion. Candle demonstration only, not a medieval lamp reconstruction.
- U.S. EPA, [Candles and Incense as Potential Sources of Indoor Air Pollution](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1009D5G.TXT): full indexed NEPIS text read; it identifies incomplete combustion, wick length, composition, and drafts as sooting factors. It gives no universal wick setting and does not predict an individual lamp.

The authoring fragment adds three claims with RU/EN localizations. Hoop fit and wick use are explicitly bounded transfers (`inferred`), rather than direct evidence for medieval objects. It intentionally makes no claim of water safety, dissolved-substance removal, a successful vessel repair, or a fixed wick adjustment.
