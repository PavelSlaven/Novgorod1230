# Transport operations — source-backed candidates

**Status:** research only. Nothing here self-approves a production fact or
changes matrix/code. The earlier `population-transport-practices.md` TP
snippet is withdrawn and is not used. Existing corpus already owns boat,
cart, sledge and oar forms, boat construction/repair and cart street use;
these candidates concern evidenced operational contexts only.

## Exclusions

- Letter 685 (1160–1180) mentions preparations for a trip «за Волок», but
  does not name a method of crossing a portage. It does **not** support
  boat-hauling, horse portage, road maintenance, or a canonical route.
- The chronicle's boat episodes opened in the 1950 scholarly edition are
  military and/or outside the requested Novgorod-1230 household-operation
  scope; they are not promoted as ordinary navigation rules.
- Neither the letters nor Vasil'ev authorize a present boat/cart/sledge,
  particular river passage, route topology, compulsory transport profession,
  player access, or an outcome of navigation.

## Result

Five candidates add operation-level evidence without duplicating transport
forms. Two are direct XIII-century Novgorod records (TO-02 and TO-03); the
other three are earlier local continuity evidence and require the stated
inferred/medium temporal qualifier. Independent verification remains required.
