The owner set this rule for everything in the game (D40, #133; written into PC §9.1).

- **Inclusion criterion.** Include whatever exists in the world, or by the logic of reality must exist: things, animals, plants, people, roles, customs, norms. Anticipated usefulness is not a criterion — the player's actions are free. Owner's example: «игрок может просто поймать мышь и съесть».
- **Allowed exclusions:** anachronism, physical impossibility, duplicate. Contradictions are fixed, not dropped. Foreign things are not excluded, since they could be imported.
- **Where sources are silent**, fill with basis `logical_necessity` (D38): record a derivation rule, add an anachronism guard, and never present it as a source. The world should be broad and diverse — Rus of the 13th century was a trade hub; don't insist on strict historical proof.
- **Breadth separately from depth.** Breadth is maximal. Depth means a uniform set of properties sufficient for any action.
- **Any use goes through properties** (physical and social), not through lists of uses. Wide category trees get default properties as a safety net.
- The owner's master archive `Novgorod1230_MASTER_ARCHIVE_v1` (on servak `/srv/novgorod-work/data/master-archive/unpacked/`) is allowed as a draft source for `logical_necessity` (D39). Its prices are model_only.

  - costume dataset v1;

  Other Downloads content stays off-limits unless the owner names it.

**Why:** in a free-action game any excluded entity later becomes either a narrator invention or a refusal. The prose test showed gaps cause inventions. I once told Codex to unbind «non-game» small mammals and raptors, and the owner reversed it.

**How to apply:**
- In every data task and review, an exclusion or «unbound» must carry a reason from the closed list. «Not needed» is a blocking review finding.
- In task prompts, ask for breadth and diversity and for the `basis` and `derivation` fields.
