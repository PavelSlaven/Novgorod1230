## Approved process premises

All ten claims are APPROVE. They are bounded qualitative mechanisms, not recipes, quantities, historical materialization, or guarantees.

- `claim:static-forge-welding-requires-heated-pressed-interface` — TWI describes forge welding as heating metal to a threshold and hammering or pressing it together; ASM describes seating faying surfaces, heating, then additional pressure. [TWI](https://www.twi-global.com/technical-knowledge/faqs/what-is-forge-welding), [ASM International](https://www.asminternational.org/results/-/journal_content/56/ASMHBA0001377/BOOK-ARTICLE/). This is conditional on the forge-welding process; it neither makes all metals forge-weldable nor attests a metal, flux, tool, or period.
- `claim:static-forge-welding-surface-oxide-can-degrade-join` — TWI says oxidation of welding surfaces can create a poor-quality weld. [TWI](https://www.twi-global.com/technical-knowledge/faqs/what-is-forge-welding). The claim remains a possible quality mechanism, not a predicted join outcome.
- `claim:static-forge-welding-pressure-depends-on-temperature-and-material` — TWI explicitly ties needed pressure to heating temperature, hardness, and strength of materials. [TWI](https://www.twi-global.com/technical-knowledge/faqs/what-is-forge-welding). No value, alloy suitability, or historical availability follows.
- `claim:static-wool-moisture-heat-mechanical-action-can-felt` — the Canadian Conservation Institute states wool’s overlapping scales can interlock with moisture, heat, and mechanical action. [Canadian Conservation Institute](https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/preventive-conservation/guidelines-collections/textiles-costumes.html). It does not prescribe a textile process or result.
- `claim:static-lubricant-film-can-separate-moving-surfaces` — SKF describes the lubricant film as separating moving parts. [SKF Evolution](https://evolution.skf.com/at-the-boundary-between-lubrication-and-wear-part-1/). Separation is conditional, not guaranteed.
- `claim:static-asperity-contact-can-cause-local-stress-and-wear` — SKF describes asperity contact through oil as producing high local stresses and wear or plastic deformation. [SKF Evolution](https://evolution.skf.com/at-the-boundary-between-lubrication-and-wear-part-1/). Actual wear depends on contact conditions.
- `claim:static-nonhydraulic-lime-can-harden-by-carbonation` — Historic England describes calcium hydroxide absorbing atmospheric carbon dioxide and converting back to calcium carbonate. [Historic England](https://historicengland.org.uk/education/training-skills/training/webinars/recordings/webinar-on-mortars-for-conservation-part-1-history-and-materials/). This does not attest lime, limestone, kiln fuel, or practice at a particular place or time.
- `claim:static-hydraulic-lime-can-initially-set-with-water` — Historic England describes water forming interlocking calcium-silicate- and calcium-aluminate-hydrate crystals responsible for hydraulic lime’s initial set. [Historic England](https://historicengland.org.uk/education/training-skills/training/webinars/recordings/webinar-on-mortars-for-conservation-part-1-history-and-materials/). No formulation, curing result, availability, or construction performance is asserted.
- `claim:static-wood-ash-ph-raising-effect-varies-by-sample` — University of Maine Extension reports substantial variation among wood-ash calcium-carbonate equivalents and hence pH-raising effectiveness. [University of Maine Cooperative Extension](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/wp-content/uploads/sites/52/2015/04/2279.pdf). This is not an application or agricultural recommendation.
- `claim:static-wood-ash-can-irritate-skin-and-eyes` — University of Maine Extension states ash can irritate skin and eyes. [University of Maine Cooperative Extension](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/wp-content/uploads/sites/52/2015/04/2279.pdf). The claim makes no diagnosis, severity, threshold, treatment, or outcome assertion.

The full assembled base has an existing broad lime-transformations premise, but not either reviewed carbonation or hydraulic-initial-set causal relation; it is therefore not a meaningful duplicate. The remaining reviewed relations have no material duplicate in that base.
