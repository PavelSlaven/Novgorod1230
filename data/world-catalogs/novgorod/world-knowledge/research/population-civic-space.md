# Civic defence and public-space — evidence candidates, not production

Scope: material categories and maintenance/work contexts historically compatible with Novgorod and north-west Rus, roughly 1100–1300. None of these rows authorises a canonical gate, wall, market, pier, route, crowd, or topological connection in a scene.

## Sources actually read

- **CS-S1 — G. S. Lebedev, “Торговая сторона Ярославова Дворища,”** in *Эпоха викингов в Северной Европе и на Руси*, full text hosted by [Arheologija.ru](https://arheologija.ru/torgovaya-storona-yaroslavova-dvorishha/). Relevant passages: Vladimir’s 1044–1052 construction of Detinets fortifications; Mstislav’s 1116 enlargement of its wood-and-earth walls; the early-XII earth rampart of the Okolny town; and the named Trade Side built environment.
- **CS-S2 — IA RAS, “О зимних работах Новгородской экспедиции,”** [excavation report](https://archaeolog.ru/press/articles/o-zimnikh-rabotakh-novgorodskoy-ekspeditsii-ia-ran). Relevant passage: large medieval storage rooms along the Volkhov bank, boat fragments and iron clamps connected with river-vessel repair.
- **CS-S3 — IIMK RAS, N. V. Khvoshchinskaya, “Раскопки мысовой части Новгородского (Рюрикова) городища,”** [official field-research report](https://www.archeo.ru/field-research/annotations/raskopki-myisovoj-chasti-novgorodskgo-ryurik-12aee). Read in full. It directly documents the archaeological reading of log courses, cross walls, stakes and clay in a timber-and-earth defensive construction, but its absolute date is 858–862. It is used only as a material-construction comparison, **not** as proof of a XII–XIII installation.

## Disposition

CS-01–07 are candidate historical compatibility relations for independent verification. CS-08 is deliberately not a candidate. The strongest sources establish categories and dated contexts, not the presence of any specific civic object in the game. Avoid later stone Kremlin walls and towers: they are outside the intended XI–XIII material envelope.
