# Candidate set: social institutions, household, exchange and religion

**Status:** research candidates for independent verification. They are not
approved claims, law, gameplay permissions, NPC profiles, or proof of a
current person, object, debt, family relation, authority, obligation, market,
church, calendar event, or literacy.

## Reopened primary-source register

- **SIC-01 — Birch-bark letter №718**, [Old Russian Birch-Bark Documents
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/718/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1220–1240, stratigraphic XIII century, other dating preference
  1180s–1210s. Corpus classifies it as a register of income/payments from
  Gorodets Bezhetsky; the surviving text enumerates accounting and commodity
  entries. Exact date and fiscal regime remain unresolved.
- **SIC-02 — Letter №531**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/531/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1200–1220, first-half-XIII stratigraphic envelope. A private
  letter from Anna to her brother Klimyata asking protection against an
  alleged unfair accusation. It is a single addressed message, not a family or
  judicial rule.
- **SIC-03 — Letter №705**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/705/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1200–1220, stratigraphically early XIII. A private letter from
  Domazhir to his sister's husband Yakov making a contingency-bound request
  about sending/returning a referent whose reading is not securely resolved.
  It records neither custody, inheritance, nor compulsory care.
- **SIC-04 — Letter №715**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/715/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1220–1240, first-half-to-mid-XIII stratigraphic envelope.
  Corpus classifies a written charm-prayer against fever, with invocation of
  the Mother of God. It establishes neither treatment efficacy nor any
  person's belief or practice.
- **SIC-05 — Letter №778**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/778/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1200–1220, stratigraphic 1190s–1210s. An alphabet exercise.
  This is direct evidence for one instructional written text, not a literacy
  rate or an inference that an actor can read or write.
- **SIC-06 — Letter №739**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/739/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1120–1140. Corpus describes Glebok's instruction to Volokhans
  to pay a church due. This is only broad 1100–1300 documentary compatibility:
  it is not a 1230 institution, rate, authority, or enforceable demand.
- **SIC-07 — Letter №907**, [same institutional
  corpus](https://gramoty.ru/birchbark/document/show/novgorod/907/): Novgorod;
  conventional 1100–1120. A letter to posadnik Gyuryata about investigating a
  theft. It supports a broad documentary category of addressing an authority
  about an allegation, not a 1230 court procedure, witness rule, office power,
  or outcome.

## Exclusions and coverage gaps

- Later records deliberately excluded from 1230 claims: trade instructions №68
  (1240–1260), church property letter №413 (1400–1410), church staffing
  petition №963 (1410–1420), and judicial protocol №154 (1420–1430).
- No source recovered here proves a market rule, price, fixed measure, coin
  standard, property title, inheritance, witness procedure, oath, arrest,
  court composition, legal status taxonomy, enforceable household obligation,
  calendar observance, or a named NPC's occupation/relation.
- Existing №76 is only evidence for the historical hired-work category. It is
  not enough to assign a role-bound fact to a particular NPC without an
  independently approved profile mapping and `knowledge_access` gate.
