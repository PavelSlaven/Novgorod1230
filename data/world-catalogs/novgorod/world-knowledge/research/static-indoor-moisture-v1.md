# Static indoor moisture v1

Research-only candidates, outside the production descriptor; independent review pending.

Existing humidity/airflow/drying, microbial growth and grain moisture claims already cover storage effects. They do not directly supply indoor combustion moisture or air-movement/condensation mitigation. No new mold, drying-time or food-safety claims are needed.

Source: US EPA, [A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home), updated February 18, 2026; inspected September 4, 2026. Under “Moisture and Mold Prevention and Control Tips”:

- “Actions that will help to reduce humidity” identifies combustion appliances, including stoves, as possible indoor water-vapour sources unless vented outside.
- “Actions that will help prevent condensation” lists increased ventilation or air movement as mitigation.

These two conditional premises support shelter/storage and burning contexts. They do not establish an appliance, medieval installation, fuel composition, ventilation, humidity, temperature, condensation event or successful drying. No modern equipment recommendation, numerical threshold or treatment advice is imported into gameplay.
