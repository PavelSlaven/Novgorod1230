Contemporary ranges, abundance, law, exact local dates and scene presence are not promoted. Facts remain domain-internal; a universal biological property does not assert that an animal exists in the current place or is known to an actor.

Browser harness hung reproducibly; official troubleshooting was read and user informed. The following sources were opened through the approved web fallback. Only cited biological sections were used. No treatment advice, exact yields, probabilities or local population sizes.
