## Hide preparation and effluent

[UNIDO, *Introduction to treatment of tannery effluents*](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/46/70/4670676/KRAL_AGR_AIT_BGD_2011_101002_001.pdf) distinguishes soaking/general effluent from beam-house effluent and identifies water from fleshing and splitting as a distinct effluent class. [EPA’s leather-tanning development document](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=00000W73.TXT) states that soak/wash precedes fleshing and that fleshings can be isolated as solid waste. These modern technical sources support only general causal relations: wet preparation can be coupled to removable residue and liquid waste; they do not supply a medieval process or chemical recipe.

The candidate’s separate-path claim is deliberately editorial: if a scene has both work water and water reserved for a clean task, distinct vessels, surfaces, or flow paths can avoid direct mixing. It does not assert that any named historical yard had such a layout.

## Fish smoking and preservation

[FAO, *Fish and fish products*](https://www.fao.org/4/x5434e/x5434e0f.htm) describes smoking’s preservation as drying plus smoke compounds, with heat also affecting microbial growth; it says salting may be combined with drying and that effectiveness depends on process conditions. [FAO/Codex, *Code of Practice for Fish and Fishery Products*](https://www.fao.org/4/i2382e/i2382e.pdf) defines smoke drying as combined smoking and drying and treats salt, time, and temperature as controlled factors. This supports a conditional combined-process claim, not a recipe or a fixed storage result.

[FAO, *Smoked Fish Recommended Practice for Retailers*](https://www.fao.org/4/x5895e/x5895e01.htm) says colour is an unreliable guide to quality. Thus smoke smell, colour, or visible smoke can describe a scene but cannot establish safety or a storage outcome. No diagnosis follows from their presence or absence.

## Source limits

All technical sources are modern and universal-process evidence, not direct evidence of Novgorod practice in 1230. The only time/place-specific candidate is marked editorial and limited to ordinary composition.
