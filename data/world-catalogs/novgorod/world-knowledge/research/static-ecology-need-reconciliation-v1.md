## Dispositions

The broad categories in cartography are not used as evidence; every closure above names
claim-level premises. Species-specific additions remain open only when a future potential
consumer needs a particular taxon/process relation. No residual permits local presence,
materialization, inventory, NPC behavior, weather, current health or exact mechanics.
