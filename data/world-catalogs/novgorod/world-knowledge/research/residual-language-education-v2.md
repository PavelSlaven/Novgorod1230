## New source and scope

This additive candidate shard leaves v1 immutable for independent review. It reopens the official [Russian Science Foundation summary of A. Gippius’s lecture](https://www.rscf.ru/news/humanitarian-sciences/slits-k-yvanou/) (14 November 2022), which presents the expert reading of Novgorod birch-bark material. It uses three direct portions: the overview of household graphic practice, Gramota 1144, and Gramota 1157.

## Relation to existing evidence

The existing v5 `Михея Трасавиче` record remains the only direct near-1230 patronymic-like named form already in production; it is not duplicated. V2 adds a distinct XIII-century husband-derived female form and a direct source statement about written address and graphic constraints.

## Remaining source limit

The reopened scholarly and catalogue sources do not directly establish workshop apprenticeship, master–learner relations, observation, practice, correction, or a craft-skill transfer system. Existing universal learning premises and the educational text corpus remain separate. No v2 claim turns a writing exercise, tool, child, or occupation into proof of an apprenticeship system.
