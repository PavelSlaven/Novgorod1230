# Household dependant care and reciprocal care — bounded research

**Status:** research candidates only; no self-approval and no production
authoring. The purpose is a qualified Old Rus family/dependant substrate, not
an assertion that a present actor has kin, children, a widow, a household,
property, a duty or a legal remedy.

## Sources independently opened and read

1. Moscow State University, [*Пространная Русская
   Правда*](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/prp_t.htm), electronic text
   after M. N. Tikhomirov, *Пособие для изучения Русской Правды* (1953).
   The full public text was opened. The displayed text is explicitly the
   Troitsky copy of the second half of the XIV century; the relevant passages
   are §§93, 95 and 96 under “А се о заднице” / “О жене, аже
   ворчеться седети.”
2. M. N. Tikhomirov, [“Списки и происхождение редакций Русской
   Правды”](https://www.hist.msu.ru/ER/Etext/RP/intro.htm), in the same MSU
   scholarly electronic edition, pp. 16–19, 28–29. The introduction explains
   the multi-redaction/later-copy problem: the oldest extant Kormchaia copy
   with the text is c. 1282 in Novgorod; the Synodal and Troitsky copies share
   a prototype earlier than the last quarter of the XIII century; the Troitsky
   manuscript itself is later. It does **not** license treatment as a simple,
   uniformly current Novgorod-1230 statute.

## Historical applicability

All candidates are direct as passages of the *Extended Russkaya Pravda* but
only **inferred / medium** as a broad 1100–1300 Old Rus / Novgorod-compatible
normative-family envelope. They are `domain_internal_only`. They are not
`historically_attested` present household facts, nor automatic local law,
enforcement, kinship or care duties at 1230.

## Atomic candidates

| ID | Subject → predicate → object | Candidate wording (RU / EN) | Exact textual anchor | Limits and gameplay use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HHO-01 | minor children unable to care for themselves → may be placed with → the nearest relative, together with house and goods | **RU:** Пространная Русская Правда предусматривает, что если после смерти отца в доме остаются малые дети, ещё не способные сами о себе заботиться, а мать выходит замуж, ближайшему родственнику передаются дети, дом и имущество до тех пор, пока дети не смогут справляться сами. **EN:** The Extended Russkaya Pravda provides that when small children remain in a house after their father’s death and are not yet able to care for themselves, and their mother remarries, the nearest relative receives the children, house and goods until the children are able to manage for themselves. | §93: “Аже будуть в дому дети мали, а не джи ся будуть сами собою печаловати, а мати имъ поидеть за мужь, то кто имъ ближии будеть, тому же дати на руце и с добыткомь и с домомь, донеле же возмогуть”. | A textual, conditional guardianship model only. It does not establish that every child had a guardian, that kin are available/trustworthy, a current orphan, adoption, residence, authority, property inventory, duration, or enforceable Novgorod procedure. Useful only once a dependant relationship and relevant state are independently established. |
| HHO-02 | caretaker holding children’s goods → returns → the principal goods; caretaking and feeding are expressly named | **RU:** Та же норма различает основное имущество детей, подлежащее возврату, и приращение/доход, оставляемые держателю потому, что он кормил детей и заботился о них. **EN:** The same rule distinguishes the children’s principal goods, which are to be returned, from gain left to the holder because that person fed and cared for the children. | §93: “а истыи товаръ воротить имъ, а прикупъ ему собе, зане кормилъ и печаловалъся ими”; it also says losses from the goods are owed back to the children. | Do not normalize a formula for maintenance, profit, accounting, damages, custody, property title or a present claim. The relation supports only a qualified historical distinction between dependant care and another person’s holding of their goods. Exact assets, gains, loss and remedy belong to state/law owners. |
| HHO-03 | feeding/caring for a mother → is named as → a succession-relevant relation in a no-testament case | **RU:** В норме о материнской доле при отсутствии распоряжения назван тот, у кого мать жила во дворе и кто её кормил. **EN:** In the rule on a mother’s portion, where there is no disposition, the text names the person in whose household the mother lived and who fed her. | §96: “безъ языка ли оумреть, то оу кого будеть на дворе была и кто ю кормилъ, то тому взяти.” | The passage names a narrow succession condition; it is not proof of universal filial support, compulsory co-residence, affection, gender roles, modern elder-care, an actual mother, a household, food stock, inheritance result or applicable local court. It may ground a conditional, already-established household care relation only. |

## Exclusions

- No candidate says that hospitality, mutual aid, maintenance or inheritance is
  mandatory ordinary behaviour.
- These passages do not settle a concrete family status, person’s age, widow,
  remarriage, kin availability, household composition, asset, guardian,
  successor, consent, capacity, jurisdiction or remedy.
- The current corpus’s kin-request, spousal-complaint and priest-child-baptism
  records are separate evidence and have not been duplicated.
- A verifier should reject any proposed normalization that silently upgrades
  this layered legal text into the executable law of Novgorod in 1230.
