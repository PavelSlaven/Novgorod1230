## Scoped research-only candidates

### ENV-01 — freeze–thaw sediment disaggregation

- **RU:** «При циклах замерзания и оттаивания расширяющийся лёд в грунте может
  разрушать ранее связанный осадочный материал; после таяния разрыхлённый грунт
  может быть более подвержен эрозии.»
- **EN:** “Across freeze–thaw cycles, expanding ice in soil can break up previously
  consolidated sediment; after thaw, loosened soil can be more vulnerable to erosion.”
- **Source / exact anchor:** U.S. Geological Survey, *Freeze-Thaw cycles can cause
  bank erosion*, **Detailed Description**. [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/freeze-thaw-cycles-can-cause-bank-erosion).
- **Evidence boundary:** direct official geomorphology explanation; distinct from the
  pack’s rock-fracture claim. No claim about any actual bank collapse, rate, route,
  weather event, sediment type or accessibility.

### ENV-02 — flood can leave recognizable high-water-mark types

- **RU:** «Паводок может оставлять отметки высокой воды, включая отложившийся ил,
  скопившийся на препятствиях мусор и эрозионные следы.»
- **EN:** “A flood can leave high-water marks including deposited mud, snagged debris,
  and erosion scars.”
- **Source / exact anchor:** U.S. Geological Survey, *High-Water Marks*, **A Look at
  the Evidence**; also **Rapid-High-Water Marks**. [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/water-resources/science/high-water-marks).
- **Evidence boundary:** observation-category relation only. It establishes neither
  that an observed mark was made by a particular event nor the event’s peak,
  chronology, cause, magnitude, local water level or future risk.

### ENV-03 — porous-surface stain needs corroboration

- **RU:** «На пористом материале линия окрашивания после паводка может оказаться
  выше фактического пика воды из-за капиллярного впитывания; для оценки нужны
  подтверждающие отметки, в особенности на близких непористых поверхностях.»
- **EN:** “On porous material, a post-flood stain line can lie above the actual water
  peak because of capillary wicking; assessing it requires corroborating marks,
  especially on nearby nonporous surfaces.”
- **Source / exact anchor:** U.S. Geological Survey, *High-Water Marks*, **Tranquil
  High-Water Marks — Mud Lines**, paragraphs beginning “Porous materials such as
  wood and concrete…” and “To ensure accuracy…”. [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/water-resources/science/high-water-marks).
- **Evidence boundary:** this is a limitation on interpreting a particular type of
  mark, not a generic ability to measure floods. It gives no actual mark, material,
  elevation, confidence value, survey, scene flood or conclusion.

### ENV-04 — atmospheric conditions affect smoke transport and dispersion

- **RU:** «Перемещение и рассеивание дыма от огня зависят от атмосферных условий,
  включая ветер, устойчивость и неустойчивость воздуха.»
- **EN:** “Movement and dispersion of smoke from a fire are affected by atmospheric
  conditions including wind, stability, and instability.”
- **Source / exact anchor:** National Weather Service, *Fire Weather Glossary*, entry
  **“Smoke Transport”**. [NWS](https://www.weather.gov/okx/fireweatherglossary).
- **Evidence boundary:** qualitative atmospheric mechanism only. It does not supply a
  fire, smoke, weather state, plume direction, distance, concentration, exposure,
  visibility or outcome.

### ENV-05 — inversion can retain smoke near the ground

- **RU:** «Приземная температурная инверсия может удерживать дым на низком уровне
  и препятствовать его достаточному подъёму для разбавления или переноса ветром.»
- **EN:** “A surface-based temperature inversion can trap smoke at low levels and
  prevent sufficient lofting for dilution or wind transport.”
- **Source / exact anchor:** National Weather Service, *Wisconsin Annual Operating
  Plan*, **Appendix B — Smoke Management/HYSPLIT Requests**, paragraph beginning
  “A temperature inversion would serve to trap smoke…”.
  [NWS PDF](https://www.weather.gov/media/dlh/Firewx/2019_WI_AOP_Public_Final.pdf).
- **Evidence boundary:** no inversion, time of day, terrain, wind, smoke source,
  room, exposure concentration, toxic effect, travel direction or player-safe place
  is established. It is not indoor-ventilation or fire-control guidance.

## Non-candidate boundaries

- ENV-01 does not generalize `freeze–thaw` from sediment to every soil, rock, bank or
  slope and does not predict failure.
- ENV-02–03 do not make debris a materialized resource, nor allow a player to infer a
  precise historic flood from one mark.
- Existing oxygen claims are sufficient at this need-family granularity; a future
  candidate must name a genuinely missing taxon/process relation rather than restate
  temperature, low oxygen or fish loss.
- ENV-04–05 do not close CO, optical visibility, health, fire spread or current weather
  state. Those retain their present bounded claims and code/state owners.
