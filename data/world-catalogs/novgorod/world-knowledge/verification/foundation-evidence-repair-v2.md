# Foundation evidence repair v2

## Method

This is a source-repair review, not authoring. I compared the full current
RU/EN claim text against already independently reopened, production evidence.
A replacement is proposed only when the listed evidence covers the complete
claim; a nearby material keyword is not enough.

## Unsupported whole claims

- `novgorod-wood-bone-horn-classes`: direct reading of Rybina 2006 lists
  `кости` and `дерева` among the collection materials, but does not list
  horn/antler. Kolchin 1968 is specifically a catalogue of wooden objects.
  Thus neither supplies the required three-class coverage.
- `woodworked-objects-and-waste`: direct reading of Kolchin 1968 establishes
  wooden objects and their manufacturing techniques; Rybina 2006 establishes
  leather offcuts, not wood chips, shavings, sawdust, or another woodworking
  residue. Desyatinny remains evidence of a different material-work waste.

Both remain `NEEDS_REVIEW`; partial evidence must not be substituted.
