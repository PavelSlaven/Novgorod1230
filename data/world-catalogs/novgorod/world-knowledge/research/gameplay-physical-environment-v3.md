## Independently read modern anchors

1. [NIST, *Fire Dynamics Simulator Technical Reference Guide*, §7.10
   “Visibility”](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/IR/nistir6784e2002.pdf).
   Smoke attenuates transmitted light; the guide relates extinction to smoke
   particulate density and says the visible distance varies with the extinction
   coefficient and object type.  This supports qualitative obscuration, not a
   scene smoke concentration, toxicity, escape outcome, or exact range.
2. [USGS Upper Midwest Environmental Sciences Center, “Life
   Forms”](https://www.umesc.usgs.gov/data_library/vegetation/graphical/veg_help.html).
   Its definitions distinguish submergent plants, rooted floating-leaf species
   (roots and stems in sediment; leaves/flowers at surface), and emergent
   species (roots in sediment; stem/leaves/flowers above water).
3. [University of Florida IFAS Extension, *Freshwater Ornamental Aquatic Plant
   Production*, “Basic aquatic plant types”](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/FA251).
   It distinguishes emergent, floating-leaved, submersed, and floating plants;
   notes that some submerged plants are anchored by roots/rhizomes/stolons and
   that free-floating plants are not substrate-attached.  The source itself
   warns morphology is diverse, so categories are not a complete species key.
4. [University of Alaska Fairbanks Sea Grant, *Ropes for Ordinary Vessel
   Use*, “Rope Maintenance”](https://seagrant.uaf.edu/bookstore/boatkeeper/rope.pdf).
   It describes chafe/abrasion, prior stress, bends, knots and splices as
   strength-relevant and calls for condition inspection.  It is contemporary
   vessel guidance with modern rope materials; it does not establish medieval
   cordage construction, a specific line, safe load, interval, or repair
   practice.
5. [U.S. Army TM 3-34.86 / Marine Corps MCRP
   3-17.7J](https://rdl.train.army.mil/catalog-ws/view/100.ATSC/1676C9DD-7ADF-4E41-9130-6BCEE7BBC5A9-1343151324082/tm3_34x86.pdf),
   §§1-12–1-15, identifies Manila/sisal fibre ropes and says knots and sharp
   bends can reduce strength. Its tables, safety factors and any transfer to
   historical hemp are excluded.

## General unseen probes

- A player sees a smoky shed doorway: PE-01 can limit optical clarity only;
  scene state and body/hazard owners decide whether smoke exists and what it
  does.
- A player pushes through floating leaves: PE-02 distinguishes form/attachment
  questions, but does not materialize plants or decide resistance/route access.
- A player hauls a wet, frayed line around a post: current friction claims
  reject unconditional hold; PE-03, if later independently accepted, adds
  condition-dependent loss of strength without inventing a load calculation.
- A broad plank is pulled through water: use existing drag/buoyancy claims;
  no new fluid-shape candidate is needed.

## Deliberate exclusions

- No historical Novgorod aquatic-plant use, rope-making routine, knot type, or
  boat practice is inferred from these modern sources.
- No candidate turns visibility into a numeric range or smoke into a toxic or
  lethal event.
- No candidate duplicates the existing friction, drag, or buoyancy premises.
